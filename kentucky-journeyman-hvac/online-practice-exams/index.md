### State Requirements

Kentucky regulates heating, ventilation, and air conditioning professionals under the Commonwealth's HVAC licensing laws and administrative regulations.

The Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction, through its Division of HVAC, oversees the state's HVAC licensing program.

A Kentucky journeyman HVAC mechanic is authorized to perform work involving air conditioning, warm-air heating, ventilation, equipment startup, and duct systems within the scope established by Kentucky law.

Journeyman HVAC mechanics must work under the supervision of a licensed Master HVAC Contractor.

State licensing requirements address qualifying experience, approved training, examination completion, and the documentation necessary to obtain the appropriate credential.
