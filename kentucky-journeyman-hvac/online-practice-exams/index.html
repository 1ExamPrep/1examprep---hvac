Exam Details

The Kentucky Journeyman HVAC examination is administered by ProV, Inc. through Kentucky's HVAC licensing examination program. The examination evaluates technical knowledge related to heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration, mechanical installation, electrical components, and system maintenance.

  • Exam Name: Kentucky Journeyman HVAC
  • Testing Provider: ProV, Inc.
  • Licensing Authority: Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction, Division of HVAC
  • Exam Format: Computer-based examination
  • Number of Questions: 60
  • Time Limit: 3 hours
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Online Practice Access: 3 months

The ProV Journeyman HVAC examination outline identifies eight primary subject areas:

  • General Knowledge – 14 Questions: Fundamental HVAC concepts, industry terminology, mechanical principles, and general technical knowledge.
  • Systems and Sizing – 6 Questions: HVAC system characteristics, equipment sizing concepts, and system performance considerations.
  • Equipment – 6 Questions: Heating and cooling equipment, components, and installation principles.
  • Piping – 6 Questions: Piping systems, materials, and related HVAC installation requirements.
  • Refrigeration Systems – 9 Questions: Refrigeration principles, components, system operation, and cooling equipment.
  • Electrical – 6 Questions: Electrical concepts, HVAC controls, motors, and equipment-related electrical components.
  • Maintenance – 10 Questions: HVAC service principles, maintenance procedures, and system performance.
  • Plan Reading, Estimating, and Math – 3 Questions: Technical drawings, mathematical relationships, and basic estimating concepts.

General Knowledge is the largest examination category, with 14 questions. Maintenance follows with 10 questions, while Refrigeration Systems represents nine questions.

State Requirements

Kentucky regulates heating, ventilation, and air conditioning professionals under the Commonwealth's HVAC licensing laws and administrative regulations.

The Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction, through its Division of HVAC, oversees the state's HVAC licensing program.

A Kentucky journeyman HVAC mechanic is authorized to perform work involving air conditioning, warm-air heating, ventilation, equipment startup, and duct systems within the scope established by Kentucky law.

Journeyman HVAC mechanics must work under the supervision of a licensed Master HVAC Contractor.

State licensing requirements address qualifying experience, approved training, examination completion, and the documentation necessary to obtain the appropriate credential.

Journeyman applicants should understand the technical responsibilities associated with safe HVAC installation and service, including proper equipment operation, refrigeration system principles, mechanical code compliance, and fuel gas safety.

These subjects are relevant not only to the licensing examination but also to the professional skills required in residential and commercial HVAC work.
