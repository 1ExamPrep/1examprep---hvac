### Test Information and Study Materials

The most effective journeyman HVAC prep is built around **application** and **retrieval**. Instead of reading passively, train yourself to locate the right detail quickly and apply it correctly—because that’s what open-book testing measures.

**1) Build your “book map.”**
Spend time learning how each reference is organized. Your goal is to reduce search time and improve confidence using the books as tools.

- Review tables of contents to understand topic groupings.
- Practice index lookups for terms you see in real HVAC work.
- Use headings and subheadings to narrow down quickly once you’re in the right section.

**2) Train scenario thinking.**
Journeyman HVAC questions often describe jobsite situations. Practice identifying the system, the risk, and which book you should check first. This habit saves time and reduces confusion.

**3) Rotate through domains.**
Avoid studying only what feels easiest. Rotate through refrigeration systems, equipment behavior, maintenance, piping/fuel gas awareness, and plan reading/math so your performance improves across the full exam.

**4) Use timed drills.**
Short timed practice sets build pacing. They teach you to keep moving and prevent over-searching. After each set, review missed questions by cause:

- **Knowledge gap:** concept wasn’t clear
- **Navigation gap:** didn’t know where to find it
- **Detail gap:** missed a key condition
- **Timing gap:** spent too long searching and rushed the choice

This method improves scores faster because you fix the pattern behind the miss—not just the single question.
