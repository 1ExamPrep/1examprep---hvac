### Licensing Steps

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers mechanical journeyman licensing. Candidates pursuing an HVACR Limited Journeyman license must complete the applicable experience, application, examination, and licensing requirements.

1. **Work as a mechanical apprentice.** Gain qualifying HVACR experience while meeting Oklahoma apprentice registration requirements applicable to the work being performed.
2. **Build qualifying mechanical experience.** Accumulate the experience needed for the HVACR Limited Journeyman category.
3. **Meet the journeyman qualification requirements.** Oklahoma allows qualifying pathways involving mechanical trade experience and certain approved education combined with experience.
4. **Submit the mechanical license application.** Apply through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board for the appropriate journeyman category.
5. **Receive examination approval.** Once the application is approved, the candidate can proceed with scheduling the required examination.
6. **Take the HVACR Journeyman Limited examination.** Prepare for the technical subjects and approved open-book references before testing.
7. **Complete the licensing process.** Follow the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board requirements for obtaining and maintaining the HVACR Limited Journeyman license.

Oklahoma describes the mechanical licensing path as progressing from apprentice to journeyman and potentially to contractor. Journeymen work for an active licensed mechanical contractor and perform work within the scope of the journeyman license classification.

### State Requirements

Applicants for an Oklahoma mechanical journeyman license must be at least 18 years old and meet an approved experience or education-and-experience pathway.

One pathway requires three years of verifiable experience in the mechanical trade in the category for which the applicant is applying. Oklahoma also recognizes certain approved mechanical education programs combined with specified amounts of qualifying mechanical experience.

For Oklahoma experience to count toward licensing, mechanical apprentices generally need to maintain the required apprentice registration while gaining qualifying experience. Oklahoma defines one year of verifiable mechanical experience as 2,000 hours.

The HVACR Limited Journeyman license allows qualifying work involving cooling products, systems, or equipment, including process piping, with a cooling capacity of no more than **25 tons**.

The classification also covers qualifying heating equipment, including process piping, with a heat input of no more than **500,000 Btu/h per appliance**.

The scope also includes sheet metal, natural gas piping, and refrigeration work within the HVACR Limited Journeyman classification.

A licensed journeyman works for an active licensed mechanical contractor. Candidates should understand the limits of the HVACR Limited classification so they know how the credential fits into Oklahoma's mechanical licensing structure.
