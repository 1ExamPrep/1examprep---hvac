## How does the HVACR Limited Journeyman exam work?

The HVACR Limited Journeyman license comes from the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The HVACR Unlimited Contractor trade exam is open book: 90 questions in 240 minutes, with a 70 percent passing score (63 correct).
- From December 15, 2026 the affected HVACR exams use the 2024 International Mechanical Code and International Fuel Gas Code only.
- An active Oklahoma mechanical contractor keeps a $5,000 surety bond and at least $50,000 in general liability insurance.

### Who issues the HVACR Limited Journeyman license in Oklahoma?

It comes from the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as the HVACR Limited Journeyman license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most working HVAC contractors hold both.
