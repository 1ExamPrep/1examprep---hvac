Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman - Limited Examination is part of the mechanical licensing program overseen by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The examination is administered by PSI and evaluates technical knowledge associated with limited HVACR work.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman - Limited
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Number of Questions: 60
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 70%, or 42 correct answers

The examination evaluates six principal technical subjects: electrical knowledge and controls, natural gas, air distribution systems, refrigeration and air conditioning systems, heating systems, and safety.

Refrigeration preparation involves understanding compressors, condensers, evaporators, refrigerants, and system operation. Electrical subjects include motors, controls, protective devices, and measurements. Air distribution involves ventilation, duct systems, and airflow principles.

Heating and natural gas questions address equipment operation, gas piping, combustion air, venting, and installation requirements. Safety questions evaluate knowledge of workplace hazards and protective practices.

The examination allows an average of three minutes per question. A balanced study schedule and familiarity with the references can help candidates prepare for the testing format.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman - Limited examination is an open book test. Candidates may consult authorized reference materials during testing according to PSI examination-room requirements.

Open-book examinations require technical understanding as well as reference-navigation skills. Candidates must recognize the subject of a question, identify the appropriate reference, locate relevant information, and apply the material correctly.

State Requirements

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates mechanical licensing and establishes requirements for professionals performing covered HVACR work throughout Oklahoma.

The HVACR Limited Journeyman classification concerns authorized work involving air conditioning, refrigeration, heating, ventilation, ductwork, and associated mechanical systems within the limits established for the classification.

The limited scope includes cooling equipment up to 25 tons and heating equipment with heat input up to 500,000 BTU per hour.

Applicants must meet the applicable professional qualifications and examination requirements established by the Board. Journeyman licensing and mechanical contractor licensing are separate classifications with different responsibilities.
