Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman - Limited Examination is part of the mechanical licensing program overseen by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The examination is administered by PSI and evaluates trade knowledge associated with limited HVACR work.

The examination focuses on refrigeration and air conditioning, electrical controls, air distribution, heating systems, natural gas, and construction safety. These subjects reflect the technical knowledge involved in installing, maintaining, and repairing mechanical systems.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman - Limited
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Number of Questions: 60
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 70%, equivalent to 42 correct answers

The examination evaluates whether candidates understand the systems and procedures used in HVACR work. Preparation should address both the operation of mechanical equipment and the technical codes that govern installations.

Refrigeration and air conditioning study involves compressors, condensers, evaporators, refrigerants, system performance, and troubleshooting. Heating study covers equipment operation, combustion, controls, and ventilation requirements.

Electrical knowledge includes motors, circuits, controls, electrical measurements, and protective devices. Air distribution involves duct systems, airflow, and mechanical ventilation.

Fuel gas and safety topics are also important because HVACR work frequently involves combustion equipment, gas piping, electrical hazards, and construction jobsite responsibilities.

State Requirements

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates mechanical licensing and establishes standards for HVACR professionals performing covered work in Oklahoma.

Oklahoma's HVACR Limited Journeyman classification relates to the installation, maintenance, repair, and other authorized work involving heating, air conditioning, refrigeration, ventilation, and associated mechanical systems within the limited scope.

The limited classification addresses systems with cooling capacities of up to 25 tons and heating equipment with heat inputs of up to 500,000 BTU per hour per appliance.

Candidates pursuing journeyman licensing must satisfy the state's applicable professional qualification and examination requirements. The licensing process is administered separately from mechanical contractor business licensing.

Journeyman professionals need a working understanding of mechanical installation codes, equipment operation, troubleshooting, safe construction practices, and the technical responsibilities of their trade.
