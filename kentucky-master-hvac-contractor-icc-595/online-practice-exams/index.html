Exam Details

The Kentucky Master HVAC examination is part of the International Code Council (ICC) contractor and trades examination program. Exam 595 evaluates technical knowledge related to HVAC systems, installation requirements, mechanical codes, fuel gas systems, and plan analysis.

  • Exam Name: Kentucky Master HVAC
  • Exam Code: 595-KY
  • Exam Organization: International Code Council (ICC)
  • Testing Provider: Pearson VUE
  • Exam Format: Multiple-choice examination
  • Number of Questions: 100
  • Time Limit: 4 hours
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Online Practice Access: 3 months

The ICC examination outline identifies seven primary subject areas for Kentucky Master HVAC Exam 595:

  • HVAC Principles, Terminology, Administration, and Safety – 16%: Fundamental mechanical concepts, terminology, administrative requirements, and safety practices.
  • HVAC Installation Requirements – 14%: Equipment installation, mechanical requirements, and applicable code provisions.
  • Venting, Duct, and Combustion Air – 18%: Venting systems, air distribution, and combustion air principles.
  • Gas Piping – 8%: Fuel gas piping requirements, system installation, and safety considerations.
  • HVAC Electrical – 13%: Electrical principles, controls, components, and related HVAC applications.
  • Boilers, Exhaust Systems, and Fire Protection – 11%: Heating equipment, mechanical exhaust, and fire protection considerations.
  • Plan Analysis – 20%: Interpretation of mechanical plans, drawings, and technical construction information.

Understanding the examination outline can help you prioritize your preparation. Plan analysis represents the largest individual subject area, while venting, ductwork, combustion air, and general HVAC principles also account for substantial portions of the examination.

Because the examination covers a broad range of technical subjects, candidates benefit from studying both mechanical code provisions and the practical principles underlying HVAC equipment operation.

State Requirements

Kentucky regulates Master HVAC Contractors under the Commonwealth's HVAC licensing laws and administrative regulations. The Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction, through its Division of HVAC, oversees the licensing program.

A Kentucky Master HVAC Contractor is responsible for supervising HVAC work performed by employees and subcontractors of the licensee or the company represented by the licensee.

Applicants must meet the applicable experience qualifications, demonstrate satisfactory examination completion, and provide required documentation, including proof of insurance.

Kentucky's regulatory framework addresses the technical qualifications necessary for HVAC work as well as the legal and business responsibilities associated with operating as a master contractor.

This makes preparation for the Master HVAC examination especially important. Candidates need knowledge extending beyond individual equipment components to include mechanical safety, installation practices, system design, and interpretation of plans.

Master HVAC contractors must also understand the importance of properly supervising work, maintaining compliance with relevant requirements, and recognizing the responsibilities associated with managing an HVAC contracting business.
