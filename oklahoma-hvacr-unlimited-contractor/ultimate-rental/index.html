Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Contractor - Unlimited Examination is part of the mechanical licensing program overseen by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The examination is administered through PSI.

The examination measures knowledge of mechanical systems, refrigeration, air conditioning, heating, electrical controls, fuel gas, air distribution, and construction safety.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma HVACR Contractor - Unlimited
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Number of Questions: 90
  • Time Allowed: 240 minutes
  • Passing Score: 70%, equivalent to 63 correct answers

Major technical subject areas include electrical knowledge and controls, natural gas, air distribution systems, refrigeration and air conditioning, heating systems, and safety.

The Unlimited classification encompasses authorized mechanical work without the cooling-capacity and heating-input restrictions associated with Oklahoma's Limited HVACR classification.

Candidates should prepare for residential and commercial mechanical applications, including equipment operation, installation requirements, technical calculations, and applicable construction codes.

Mechanical contractor applicants also prepare for applicable Business and Law examination requirements. The package includes Oklahoma statutes, administrative regulations, and the NASCLA contractor business guide to support those subjects.

State Requirements

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates mechanical contractor licensing throughout Oklahoma.

An HVACR Unlimited Contractor license authorizes qualifying work involving cooling products, systems, and equipment; heating equipment; sheet metal; natural gas piping; and refrigeration within the scope established by Oklahoma law.

Applicants must meet journeyman-level requirements and have an additional year of qualifying mechanical trade experience.

Contractors must also satisfy applicable business and financial responsibility requirements. An active mechanical contractor must maintain a $5,000 corporate surety bond and evidence of at least $50,000 in commercial general liability insurance.

The Oklahoma Mechanical Licensing Act and Mechanical Industry Regulations establish additional professional responsibilities. These references are included in the package alongside materials addressing construction liens, administrative penalties, and workers' compensation.

Understanding both technical systems and contractor regulations helps candidates prepare for the examinations and develop stronger knowledge of professional business obligations.
