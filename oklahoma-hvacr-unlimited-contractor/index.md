## How does the HVACR Unlimited Contractor exam work?

The HVACR Unlimited Contractor license comes from the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The trade exam is open book: 90 questions in 240 minutes, passing score 70 percent (63 correct).
- PSI gives the exam for the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.
- The HVACR Unlimited Contractor trade exam is open book: 90 questions in 240 minutes, with a 70 percent passing score (63 correct).
- From December 15, 2026 the affected HVACR exams use the 2024 International Mechanical Code and International Fuel Gas Code only.
- An active Oklahoma mechanical contractor keeps a $5,000 surety bond and at least $50,000 in general liability insurance.
