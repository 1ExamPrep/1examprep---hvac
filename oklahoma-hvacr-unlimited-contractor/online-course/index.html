Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Contractor Unlimited examination evaluates technical knowledge involving heating, air conditioning, refrigeration, ventilation, air distribution, electrical controls, natural gas systems, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board oversees mechanical contractor licensing, while PSI administers the applicable licensing examinations.

  • Examination: Oklahoma HVACR Contractor Unlimited
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 90
  • Time Allowed: 240 minutes
  • Passing Score: 70%
  • Correct Answers Required: 63
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Mechanical Code: International Mechanical Code, 2024, with Oklahoma amendments
  • Fuel Gas Code: International Fuel Gas Code, 2024, with Oklahoma amendments

The examination includes six major technical subject areas:

  • Electrical Knowledge and Controls: 15 questions involving electrical theory, motors, circuits, and controls.
  • Natural Gas: 12 questions concerning piping, valves, connectors, regulators, supports, sizing, materials, and testing.
  • Air Distribution Systems: 25 questions involving duct sizing, design, ventilation, installation, testing, balancing, and mechanical drawings.
  • Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems: 20 questions involving refrigeration principles, equipment, system operation, and troubleshooting.
  • Heating Systems: 13 questions involving heating equipment, combustion, venting, installation, and system operation.
  • Safety: 5 questions addressing construction hazards and protective requirements.

With 240 minutes available for 90 scored questions, candidates have approximately two minutes and 40 seconds per question. Efficient reference navigation and familiarity with technical calculations are important preparation skills.

Oklahoma HVACR contractor licensing also requires the separate Mechanical Business and Law examination. This online course focuses on the unlimited HVACR trade examination and the seven listed technical references.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma HVACR Contractor Unlimited examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult approved examination materials according to PSI testing requirements.

Open-book testing requires candidates to identify the technical subject, select the appropriate reference, locate relevant information, and apply it accurately.

The International Mechanical Code supports mechanical installation, ventilation, ductwork, and refrigeration questions. The International Fuel Gas Code addresses fuel gas piping, combustion air, appliances, and venting.

The Ductulator supports air distribution calculations, while Manual N addresses commercial heating and cooling load calculations. Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning supports equipment operation, electrical controls, and refrigeration principles.

Oklahoma adopted the 2024 International Mechanical Code and International Fuel Gas Code, with applicable amendments, effective September 14, 2026.

The examination transition period runs from September 14 through December 14, 2026. During this period, affected candidates may test using either the previously adopted or newly adopted code editions.

Beginning December 15, 2026, affected examinations will use the newly adopted codes exclusively.

Approved reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed according to PSI rules. Permanent tabs are permitted, while unauthorized notes and removable temporary tabs are restricted.

Reference Books

The Oklahoma HVACR Unlimited Contractor Online Exam Prep Course focuses on seven technical study references:

  • Ductulator
    A duct-sizing calculation tool used to determine duct dimensions and evaluate airflow relationships. It supports preparation involving air distribution, duct sizing, friction loss, and practical HVAC duct design calculations.
  • International Mechanical Code, 2024
    Published by the International Code Council, this code covers mechanical installations, heating and cooling equipment, ventilation, duct systems, refrigeration, equipment access, and mechanical safety requirements.
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2024
    Addresses fuel gas piping materials, sizing, pressure regulation, valves, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, and system testing. It supports technical knowledge relevant to gas-fired heating equipment and mechanical installations.
  • Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, 22nd Edition
    A comprehensive HVACR textbook covering refrigeration cycles, equipment operation, compressors, condensers, evaporators, refrigerants, motors, electrical controls, maintenance, and troubleshooting.
  • Manual N - Commercial Load Calculation for Small Commercial Buildings, 5th Edition
    An Air Conditioning Contractors of America reference addressing heating and cooling load calculations for small commercial buildings. It supports equipment sizing, building load analysis, and commercial HVAC design knowledge.
  • Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition
    A trade mathematics textbook covering measurements, fractions, decimals, geometry, conversions, area, volume, piping calculations, and practical numerical problem-solving.
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA)
    Contains federal construction safety regulations involving protective equipment, electrical hazards, ladders, scaffolding, excavation, fall protection, tools, and workplace conditions.

These references address complementary preparation areas, including mechanical codes, fuel gas systems, duct calculations, commercial load analysis, refrigeration technology, mathematics, and workplace safety.
