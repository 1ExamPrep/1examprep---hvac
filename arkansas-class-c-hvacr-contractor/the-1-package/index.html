Exam Details

The Arkansas Class C HVACR Contractor exam requires preparation across technical, legal, regulatory, mechanical, fuel-gas, refrigeration, air-conditioning, and electrical subjects. Candidates should be prepared to work with both practical trade knowledge and detailed information contained in the listed references.

Arkansas HVAC/R Law and Arkansas HVAC/R Rules and Regulations support state-specific preparation. These references address the legal and regulatory framework connected with HVACR licensing, contractor responsibilities, classifications, regulated work, compliance, and professional obligations.

The International Fuel Gas Code, 2021 supports preparation involving fuel-gas piping, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, installation requirements, testing, inspection, and related safety provisions. Candidates should practice locating information by appliance type, piping subject, installation condition, or indexed term.

The International Mechanical Code, 2021 supports review involving mechanical equipment, ventilation, exhaust systems, ductwork, combustion air, refrigeration systems, equipment installation, and related mechanical-code requirements. Code questions may require candidates to compare definitions, tables, exceptions, notes, and connected sections.

Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, 22nd Edition supports broad technical study involving refrigeration principles, heating systems, cooling systems, system components, airflow, controls, installation, maintenance, service, and troubleshooting.

Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technology, 10th Edition provides additional coverage of refrigeration cycles, compressors, condensers, evaporators, metering devices, refrigerants, electrical controls, diagnostics, system operation, installation procedures, and service practices.

The National Electrical Code, 2020 supports preparation involving electrical requirements associated with HVACR equipment. Candidates should practice locating information concerning motors, conductors, branch circuits, disconnecting means, overcurrent protection, grounding, wiring methods, controls, and equipment installation.

The NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Arkansas, 8th Edition supports contractor business preparation. Subjects include contracts, estimating, project management, financial responsibilities, insurance, employment matters, taxes, liens, safety administration, and Arkansas contractor business practices.

Because the exam draws from several references, candidates should not rely only on memorized definitions or isolated facts. Stronger preparation combines practical knowledge with repeated use of indexes, code structures, diagrams, tables, chapter organization, and cross-references.

The Arkansas Class C HVACR Contractor exam is open book. Candidates may use approved references during the examination, subject to the current testing rules concerning permitted books, editions, highlighting, annotations, permanent tabs, bindings, and loose materials.

Every reference included with The 1 Package is highlighted and permanently tabbed. Highlighted passages direct attention to useful laws, rules, code provisions, technical procedures, definitions, diagrams, tables, electrical subjects, and business concepts.

Questions involving refrigeration cycles, system components, controls, airflow, heating equipment, cooling equipment, installation, maintenance, service, or troubleshooting may require one of the two refrigeration and air-conditioning textbooks.

Electrical questions involving motors, conductors, branch circuits, disconnecting means, overcurrent protection, grounding, controls, or HVACR equipment may require the National Electrical Code.

Questions involving contracts, estimating, financial management, insurance, project administration, employment matters, taxes, liens, or Arkansas contractor business practices may require the NASCLA Arkansas business reference.
