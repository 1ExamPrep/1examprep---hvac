The Ohio PSI HVAC Online Course offered by Pass Your HVAC Exam is designed to prepare candidates for the Ohio HVAC Contractor licensing exam administered by PSI Services LLC. How does the HVAC Contractor exam work?

The HVAC Contractor license comes from the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board (OCILB). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.
