### Exam Details

The Delaware Master HVACR Restricted – Refrigeration examination evaluates knowledge associated with the installation, maintenance, repair, and alteration of refrigeration systems and related equipment. The restricted classification covers qualifying refrigeration systems used to cool surfaces or areas for food and product preservation.

The examination contains 75 questions and allows three hours for completion. The published content outline includes the following subject areas:

- General Knowledge
- Piping
- Systems and Sizing
- Maintenance
- Testing and Inspection

General knowledge preparation should include the refrigeration cycle, heat transfer, temperature, pressure, refrigerants, tools, materials, system components, controls, safety devices, and accepted refrigeration practices. Candidates should understand how the major components work together rather than studying each component as an isolated piece of equipment.

The basic refrigeration cycle includes the compressor, condenser, metering device, and evaporator. Candidates should understand the condition and purpose of the refrigerant as it moves through each part of the system. Changes in pressure, temperature, and physical state allow the refrigerant to absorb heat from one area and reject it in another.

Piping questions may address refrigeration piping materials, line sizing, supports, insulation, fittings, joining methods, oil return, valves, service access, pressure conditions, and system protection. Candidates should understand the different purposes of suction, discharge, and liquid lines and recognize how improper piping can affect equipment performance.

Systems and sizing questions may involve compressors, condensers, evaporators, metering devices, receivers, accumulators, commercial refrigeration equipment, controls, system capacity, refrigerant flow, and component relationships. Some questions may require candidates to interpret technical diagrams, equipment information, tables, or operating conditions.

Maintenance questions may cover inspection, cleaning, lubrication, adjustment, airflow, refrigerant conditions, electrical controls, component wear, and preventive service procedures. Candidates should be able to connect operating symptoms with possible causes while evaluating the complete system.

Testing and inspection questions may involve temperature and pressure readings, leak testing, electrical checks, safety controls, airflow, equipment condition, refrigerant flow, and system operation. Understanding why a test is performed is as important as knowing the procedure itself.

### Licensing Steps

The Delaware Board of Plumbing, Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Examiners regulates Master HVACR and Master HVACR Restricted licenses. Applicants pursuing the restricted Refrigeration classification must complete the state licensing process and receive approval before taking the master examination.

1. **Select the correct classification.** Apply for the Delaware Master HVACR Restricted license and choose Refrigeration as the specialty.
2. **Create or access a DELPROS account.** Delaware professional license applications are submitted through the state’s online licensing system.
3. **Complete the application.** Provide the personal, apprenticeship, employment, experience, examination, and licensing information requested by the state.
4. **Gather supporting documentation.** Required records may include apprenticeship credentials, a journeyperson certificate, employment verification, supervised experience records, and verification of licenses held in other jurisdictions.
5. **Submit records as directed.** Certain documents may need to be completed or submitted by an employer, supervisor, apprenticeship program, school, or licensing authority.
6. **Complete any applicable equivalency requirement.** Applicants without a qualifying apprenticeship credential may need to complete the apprenticeship bypass or equivalency requirement associated with the Refrigeration specialty.
7. **Receive Board approval to test.** The Board reviews the application and supporting documentation before authorizing the applicant to take the master examination.
8. **Register for the examination.** Follow the testing provider’s registration and scheduling instructions after receiving approval.
9. **Take the examination.** Follow all current identification, reference-book, calculator, appointment, and testing-conduct requirements.
10. **Complete remaining licensing requirements.** The Board must receive the required examination result and supporting records before issuing the professional license.

Applicants should begin gathering official records early. Apprenticeship documentation, employment verification, and out-of-state license records may require action from third parties. Complete documentation helps the Board review the applicant’s qualifications.

### State Requirements

Delaware offers an unrestricted Master HVACR license and several Master HVACR Restricted specialties. Refrigeration is one of the restricted classifications recognized by the state.

A Delaware Master HVACR Restricted – Refrigeration license authorizes the holder to provide services within the Refrigeration specialty. A restricted license does not provide the complete scope of authority associated with an unrestricted Master HVACR license.

Professionals who intend to provide services across several restricted HVACR specialties must pursue the license classification appropriate for their complete scope of work. License holders are responsible for remaining within the authority granted by their Delaware credential.

The qualification pathway depends on the applicant’s apprenticeship training, journeyperson credential, supervised work experience, examination requirements, and supporting documentation. Applicants using a qualifying apprenticeship pathway must provide the applicable credential and document the required experience under qualified supervision.

Applicants without a qualifying apprenticeship credential may need to document a longer period of supervised HVACR or restricted HVACR experience and complete the applicable apprenticeship bypass or equivalency requirement.

Experience records should demonstrate work relevant to refrigeration systems. Applicable experience may include equipment installation, refrigeration piping, compressors, condensers, evaporators, metering devices, commercial refrigeration equipment, controls, testing, maintenance, troubleshooting, repair, or alteration.

Applicants must hold the appropriate certification for handling regulated refrigerants when required for the work performed. The Delaware Board determines whether the applicant’s experience, training, examination results, certifications, and supporting records satisfy the licensing requirements.

A Delaware professional license is separate from a Delaware business license. A refrigeration contractor planning to operate a company may also need entity registration, tax accounts, insurance, permits, local approvals, and other operating credentials.
