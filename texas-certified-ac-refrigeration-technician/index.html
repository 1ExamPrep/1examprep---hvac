How does the Certified A/C and Refrigeration Technician exam work?

The Certified A/C and Refrigeration Technician license comes from the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  • TDLR licenses air conditioning and refrigeration contractors in two classes: Class B up to 25 tons cooling and 1.5 million BTU per hour heating, and Class A with no limit.
  • PSI gives the exams, and they are open book; since September 1, 2026 they reference the 2024 IMC, UMC and IFGC.

Is the Certified A/C and Refrigeration Technician exam open book?

Yes. The trade exam for this license is open book, with the references listed in the candidate bulletin. Highlighted and tabbed books save the most time.

Who issues the Certified A/C and Refrigeration Technician license in Texas?

It comes from the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as the Certified A/C and Refrigeration Technician license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most working HVAC contractors hold both.
