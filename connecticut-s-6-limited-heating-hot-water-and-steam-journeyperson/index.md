## How does the Limited Heating, Hot Water and Steam Journeyperson (S-6) exam work?

The Limited Heating, Hot Water and Steam Journeyperson (S-6) license comes from the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, Occupational and Professional Licensing. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Connecticut D licenses cover warm air, air conditioning and refrigeration (D-1 to D-4); S licenses cover heating, piping and cooling (S-1 to S-10).
- Each license code has its own exam, and several S exams allow approved books in the exam room.
