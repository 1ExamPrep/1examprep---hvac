### Open Book Test

The **Maryland HVACR Journeyman (PSI) examination is an open book test**. Candidates may bring approved reference publications into the examination room according to PSI's testing rules.

Approved references include the 2018 International Mechanical Code, Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning in the 21st or 22nd Edition, Manual J Residential Load Calculation in the specified eighth edition, OSHA construction safety regulations, and the ACCA Ductulator.

PSI permits reference books to contain highlighting, underlining, and approved indexing tabs. Books must not contain handwritten notes or additional loose or attached papers. Study guides are not permitted in the examination room.

The examination provider does not supply the approved references at the testing center.

### State Requirements

The Maryland Board of Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Contractors regulates HVACR licensing throughout the state.

Maryland establishes specific qualifications for the Journeyman HVACR license, including apprenticeship experience and successful completion of the licensing examination.

The established requirements include:

- **Apprenticeship Period:** Holding an HVACR apprentice license for at least four years.
- **Practical Training:** Completing at least 6,000 hours of HVACR training during the apprenticeship period.
- **Licensed Supervision:** Completing qualifying training under the direction and control of a licensed HVACR contractor.
- **Examination:** Passing the Maryland HVACR Journeyman examination with a minimum score of 70%.
- **Current Apprentice Status:** Holding an apprentice license when applying through the established journeyman licensing pathway.

Maryland law also provides for recognition of certain comparable professional training, formal education, teaching experience, and qualifying work experience when accepted by the Board.

A Journeyman HVACR license allows the license holder to provide HVACR services within the scope established by state law and under the applicable direction and control of a licensed contractor.

The ACCA Ductulator is a duct sizing slide-rule tool used to evaluate duct dimensions, airflow, and friction relationships. It supports preparation involving air distribution system design and practical duct sizing concepts.
