## How does HVAC licensing work in Oklahoma?

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB) licenses HVAC work here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The HVACR Unlimited Contractor trade exam is open book: 90 questions in 240 minutes, with a 70 percent passing score (63 correct).
- From December 15, 2026 the affected HVACR exams use the 2024 International Mechanical Code and International Fuel Gas Code only.
- An active Oklahoma mechanical contractor keeps a $5,000 surety bond and at least $50,000 in general liability insurance.

### Can you bring books into the Oklahoma exam?

Several exams in this state let you bring approved books, which is why exam-room book packages are offered. Your candidate bulletin lists the books allowed for your exam.

If you handle refrigerant, yes. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most HVAC contractors hold both.
