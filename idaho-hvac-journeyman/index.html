How does the HVAC Journeyman exam work?

The HVAC Journeyman license comes from the Idaho HVAC Board in the Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses (DOPL). The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  • The Idaho HVAC Board licenses apprentices, journeymen, specialty journeymen and contractors.
  • A contractor applicant first holds an Idaho journeyman or specialty journeyman license.

Is the HVAC Journeyman exam open book?

Several exams in this state let you bring approved books, which is why exam-room book packages are offered. Your candidate bulletin lists the books allowed for your exam.

Who issues the HVAC Journeyman license in Idaho?

It comes from the Idaho HVAC Board in the Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses (DOPL). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as the HVAC Journeyman license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most working HVAC contractors hold both.
