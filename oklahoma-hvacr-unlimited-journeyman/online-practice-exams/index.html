Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Unlimited Journeyman Exam is administered by PSI for the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Candidates must be approved by the State of Oklahoma before scheduling the examination. Once approved, candidates are responsible for scheduling their exam appointment with PSI.

The exam is computer-based and is taken through an approved PSI testing location. Candidates should bring proper identification, follow PSI examination center rules, and use only approved reference materials during the exam. The test is timed, so preparation should include both content review and practice locating information quickly in the allowed references.

The exam is designed to evaluate knowledge needed for unlimited HVACR journeyman work. Preparation should include HVAC system fundamentals, mechanical code requirements, fuel gas code requirements, refrigeration principles, air distribution, appliance installation, equipment service, system testing, maintenance, troubleshooting, controls, trade math, and OSHA construction safety.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma HVACR Unlimited Journeyman Exam is an open book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved references to the examination center. For code questions, the examination is based on the specific code editions listed for the exam.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination session. References may not be written in before or during the examination. Candidates may use permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including Post-it style notes, are not allowed and must be removed before the exam begins. Candidates may not bring additional loose papers with approved references.

For downloaded references, the material must be spiral bound, hole-punched and placed in a binder, or stapled in the left-hand corner. Candidates may use a silent, non-printing, non-programmable calculator in the examination center, and a magnifying glass may be provided upon request.

Because this is an open book examination, preparation should include reference navigation. Candidates should practice locating information in the International Mechanical Code, International Fuel Gas Code, Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, and OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926. Knowing the subject is helpful, but knowing where to find the answer during a timed exam is also important.

Licensing Steps

Oklahoma HVACR journeyman licensing is handled through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Candidates applying for an HVACR Unlimited Journeyman license must meet the state’s eligibility requirements, submit the required application materials, receive approval to test, schedule the required examination through PSI, and complete the remaining licensing requirements after passing.

The licensing process begins with confirming that the applicant is pursuing the correct mechanical journeyman category. HVACR Unlimited Journeyman licensing is different from HVACR contractor licensing, HVACR limited journeyman licensing, refrigeration licensing, process piping, sheet metal, and natural gas piping categories. Candidates should match the license category to the type of work they intend to perform before applying.

Applicants must submit the appropriate mechanical journeyman application and supporting documentation to the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The Board reviews the application and determines whether the candidate is eligible for examination. Once approved, the candidate schedules the PSI examination and prepares using the listed references.

Passing the HVACR Unlimited Journeyman exam is an important step toward licensure. After licensure, an HVACR Unlimited Journeyman license holder may perform work within the authorized scope of the license category while working under the proper contractor classification. Journeyman-level authority is different from contractor-level authority because the journeyman license is tied to qualified trade work, while contractor licensing is tied to business responsibility and contracting authority.

Maintaining license compliance is an ongoing responsibility. HVACR journeymen should keep their license in good standing, follow Oklahoma renewal requirements, work within the correct license classification, and understand the limits of journeyman-level authority. Candidates should review current Oklahoma Construction Industries Board application materials before applying so they understand documentation, experience, examination, and work-scope requirements.

State Requirements

Oklahoma mechanical journeyman licensing is regulated by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The HVACR Unlimited Journeyman license is one of the mechanical journeyman categories recognized by the state.

An HVACR Unlimited Journeyman license is tied to performing HVACR work within the unlimited journeyman scope while working under the proper contractor category. This classification is different from a contractor license because it authorizes qualified trade work rather than independent contractor-level business authority.

Applicants for a mechanical journeyman license must be at least 18 years old and meet one of the approved experience or education pathways. Oklahoma lists a standard pathway of three years of verifiable experience in the mechanical trade category applied for. The state also recognizes approved education pathways combined with reduced experience periods, equivalent verifiable military experience, qualifying out-of-state licensure, and other category-specific qualifications where applicable.

Experience gained in Oklahoma generally requires apprentice registration on file with the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board for the experience to be considered verifiable. Verifiable experience must be mechanical work as defined under the applicable law and rules. Applicants should prepare complete documentation before applying because the Board determines eligibility before the candidate may schedule the examination.

The HVACR Unlimited Journeyman license is different from the HVACR Unlimited Contractor license. A journeyman license is tied to performing HVACR work in the field within the authorized scope, while a contractor license is tied to contractor-level business authority and responsibility. Candidates should make sure they are applying for the correct category before purchasing study materials or scheduling an exam.

These areas affect both exam preparation and professional work in the field.
