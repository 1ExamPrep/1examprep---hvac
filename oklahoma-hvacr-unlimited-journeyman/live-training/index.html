Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman Unlimited examination evaluates a candidate’s knowledge of the installation, maintenance, repair, fabrication, alteration, and extension of air conditioning, refrigeration, heating, and ventilating systems. The scope includes ductwork within complete systems that are unlimited in horsepower or tons, along with related piping, vessels, ducts, insulation, and associated HVAC/R components while employed by an HVAC/R contractor.

The current examination contains 70 scored questions. Candidates receive 180 minutes, or three hours, to complete the test. A passing score of 70% is required, which equals 49 correct answers.

The examination is divided into six major technical categories:

  • Electrical Knowledge and Controls – 15 questions: General electrical theory and knowledge, motors, DC circuits, AC circuits, and controls.
  • Natural Gas – 10 questions: Piping valves, connectors, accessories, hangers and supports, sizing, materials, testing, installation, and assembly.
  • Air Distribution Systems – 16 questions: Duct definitions, sizing and design, duct assembly and installation, hangers and supports, duct materials and shapes, insulation, fire and smoke control, hoods and exhaust systems, testing and balancing, ventilation requirements, plans, and symbols.
  • Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems – 16 questions: Refrigeration theory, refrigerants, equipment sizing and design, equipment and components, piping, installation, system operation, troubleshooting, and maintenance.
  • Heating Systems – 10 questions: Heating theory and system types, combustion air, vents and chimneys, equipment sizing, heating equipment, installation, troubleshooting, maintenance, hydronics, and hydronic piping.
  • Safety – 3 questions: Personal protective equipment, safety training, ventilation, first aid, hazardous materials, fall protection, lockout/tagout, ladders, scaffolds, and related safety responsibilities.

Air Distribution Systems and Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems are the two largest categories, with 16 questions each. Electrical Knowledge and Controls follows closely with 15 questions. Together, these three categories account for most of the scored examination, making them important priorities in a structured study plan.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman Unlimited examination is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their approved examination references to the testing center and should practice using those books before the examination.

The current official exam-room reference list for HVACR Journeyman Unlimited includes the International Mechanical Code, 2018 with Oklahoma revisions, International Fuel Gas Code, 2018 with Oklahoma revisions, Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, and OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926.

The Ductulator and Manual N - Commercial Load Calculation for Small Commercial Buildings, 5th Edition are included in the study-material list for this training program to support duct-sizing, airflow, commercial load-calculation, and equipment-sizing preparation. They are not listed as approved exam-room references for the current HVACR Journeyman Unlimited examination.

For code questions, candidates should use the specific code editions identified in the examination reference list. The current examination is based on the 2018 International Mechanical Code and 2018 International Fuel Gas Code for applicable code questions.

Later editions of approved non-code references are permitted under current testing rules. The 22nd Edition of Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning can therefore be used as a later edition of the approved non-code refrigeration reference.

Approved exam-room references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before testing. Permanent tabs are permitted. Temporary removable tabs such as Post-it notes are not allowed.

References may not contain written notes before or during the examination. Candidates are not permitted to bring additional loose papers with their approved references. Downloaded reference material must meet the testing provider’s binding requirements.

Candidates may also use a silent, non-printing, non-programmable calculator that meets current PSI testing rules.

Licensing Steps

  1. Gain qualifying HVAC/R experience. Build verifiable mechanical trade experience in the HVAC/R Unlimited category.
  2. Maintain apprentice registration when required. Oklahoma mechanical experience generally must be properly documented through apprentice registration unless lawfully obtained under an applicable exemption.
  3. Meet the minimum age requirement. Mechanical journeyman applicants must be at least 18 years old.
  4. Meet the experience or approved education pathway. Oklahoma provides multiple qualification paths based on mechanical trade experience and approved mechanical education.
  5. Document qualifying experience. Maintain records showing qualifying employment, apprentice registration, education, military experience when applicable, and other information needed for the license application.
  6. Apply through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Submit the Mechanical License Application for the HVAC/R Unlimited Journeyman classification.
  7. Receive examination eligibility. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board determines whether the applicant qualifies to sit for the exam.
  8. Schedule through PSI. Approved candidates schedule the Oklahoma mechanical examination with PSI.
  9. Prepare for all six technical categories. Review electrical controls, natural gas, air distribution, refrigeration and air conditioning, heating, and safety.
  10. Practice open-book reference navigation. Become familiar with the approved code books and technical references before test day.
  11. Complete the examination. The current HVACR Journeyman Unlimited exam requires a 70% passing score.
  12. Work within Oklahoma’s contractor structure after licensing. A journeyman performs authorized mechanical work while employed by the appropriate licensed contractor.

State Requirements

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates mechanical licensing, including the HVAC/R Unlimited Journeyman classification.

An applicant for an Oklahoma mechanical journeyman license must be at least 18 years of age. The standard licensing pathway requires three years of verifiable mechanical trade experience in the category for which the applicant is applying.

Oklahoma also recognizes approved education combined with field experience. For example, qualifying approved mechanical programs may reduce the amount of field experience required depending on the number of instructional hours completed.

For mechanical licensing purposes, one year of qualifying experience is generally defined as 2,000 hours of verifiable mechanical work.

Oklahoma experience intended to count toward licensing generally must be supported by proper apprentice registration unless the work was lawfully performed under an applicable exemption. Maintaining accurate experience records is important throughout the apprentice and licensing process.

The HVAC/R Unlimited Journeyman classification authorizes qualifying work involving cooling products, systems, and equipment including process piping; heating equipment including process piping; sheet metal; natural gas piping; and refrigeration.

The Unlimited classification differs from HVAC/R Limited licensing because it is not restricted to cooling equipment of 25 tons or less or heating equipment with a heat input of 500,000 Btu/h or less per appliance.

Test Information and Study Materials

A successful study strategy should combine technical knowledge with efficient reference navigation. An open-book exam still requires candidates to recognize what a question is asking, determine which reference is most likely to contain the answer, and locate the information within the available time.

Prioritize Air Distribution Systems. This category contains 16 questions. Study duct sizing and design, installation, materials, insulation, supports, ventilation, hoods, exhaust, testing and balancing, plans, and symbols. The Ductulator can be valuable for reinforcing airflow and duct-sizing concepts during preparation.

Give equal attention to Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems. This category also contains 16 questions. Review refrigeration theory, refrigerants, equipment sizing, components, piping, installation, operation, troubleshooting, and maintenance.

Strengthen Electrical Knowledge and Controls. The exam contains 15 questions in this category. Review motors, AC and DC circuits, electrical theory, control components, and how electrical systems interact with HVAC/R equipment.

Practice Natural Gas code navigation. Use the International Fuel Gas Code to find requirements involving pipe sizing, materials, supports, valves, connectors, testing, and installation.

Review Heating Systems. Study combustion air, vents and chimneys, heating equipment, equipment sizing, hydronic systems, installation, operation, troubleshooting, and maintenance.

Use Manual N for additional sizing practice. Commercial load-calculation study can strengthen understanding of equipment sizing and system-design concepts even though Manual N is not currently listed as an approved exam-room reference for this journeyman examination.

Practice under timed conditions. With 70 scored questions in 180 minutes, candidates have an average of approximately two and a half minutes per scored question. Strong pacing and efficient reference searches are important.
