Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Unlimited Journeyman examination evaluates knowledge of heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration, fuel gas systems, and related mechanical installations.

The examination is administered by PSI for the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB).

Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman Unlimited Examination Overview:

  • Examination Name: HVACR Journeyman Unlimited
  • License Category: HVAC/R Unlimited Journeyman
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Examination Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Multiple-choice
  • Number of Questions: 70
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes (3 hours)
  • Passing Score: 70%, or 49 correct answers
  • Examination Type: Open book

Exam preparation involves mechanical code requirements, refrigeration and air conditioning principles, natural gas, heating equipment, ventilation, electrical controls, and mechanical calculations.

The Oklahoma HVACR Unlimited classification covers heating and cooling work without the equipment-capacity limitations associated with the state's HVACR Limited category.

2024 Code Information: Oklahoma adopted the 2024 International Mechanical Code and International Fuel Gas Code, with state amendments, effective September 14, 2026.

For affected HVACR examinations, Oklahoma established a transition period from September 14 through December 14, 2026, during which qualifying candidates may test under either the previous or newly adopted code editions.

Beginning December 15, 2026, the examinations use the newly adopted 2024 code editions exclusively.

The two 2024 codebooks included in this package support preparation using Oklahoma's updated mechanical and fuel gas requirements.

Licensing Steps

Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman licensing is administered by the Construction Industries Board. Applicants must meet the applicable professional qualifications and complete the required examination process.

  1. Identify the License Category: Review the scope of Oklahoma's HVAC/R Unlimited Journeyman classification.
  2. Complete Qualifying Experience: Obtain the required mechanical trade experience through an eligible training or employment pathway.
  3. Maintain Apprentice Registration: Follow Oklahoma's registration requirements for qualifying mechanical apprenticeship experience.
  4. Document Experience: Prepare records of relevant employment, training, and mechanical work.
  5. Prepare the Application: Complete the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board's journeyman licensing application.
  6. Submit Supporting Materials: Provide the experience and education documentation required for eligibility.
  7. Receive Examination Approval: Obtain authorization before scheduling the examination.
  8. Prepare for Testing: Study mechanical codes, fuel gas requirements, refrigeration, mathematics, and safety.
  9. Schedule With PSI: Follow the examination registration process after approval.
  10. Pass the Trade Examination: Complete the Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman Unlimited examination.
  11. Finalize Licensure: Satisfy the remaining requirements established by the Construction Industries Board.

State Requirements

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates mechanical licensing and establishes the requirements for HVACR journeymen and contractors.

Minimum Age: Journeyman applicants must be at least 18 years old.

Standard Experience Pathway: Applicants must demonstrate three years of verifiable experience in the applicable mechanical trade category.

Educational Alternatives: Oklahoma accepts approved education combined with qualifying experience through several recognized pathways.

Apprentice Experience: Mechanical trade experience obtained in Oklahoma must satisfy applicable apprentice registration requirements.

Examination Authorization: Candidates must receive approval before registering for the journeyman examination.

Unlimited Work Scope: The category includes qualifying heating, cooling, refrigeration, process piping, sheet metal, and natural gas piping activities.

Professional Employment: Journeymen perform regulated work within the requirements applicable to licensed mechanical contractors.

Code Compliance: Mechanical installations must comply with adopted Oklahoma mechanical codes and amendments.

Safety Responsibilities: Mechanical professionals must observe applicable jobsite safety requirements when installing, maintaining, and repairing equipment.

These qualifications establish the framework for pursuing the Oklahoma HVACR Unlimited Journeyman credential.
