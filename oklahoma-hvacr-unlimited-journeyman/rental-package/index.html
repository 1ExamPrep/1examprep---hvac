The Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman Unlimited examination evaluates technical knowledge associated with heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration, mechanical equipment, and related installation practices.

The examination is administered by PSI for the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB).

Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman Unlimited Examination Overview:

  • Examination Name: Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman - Unlimited
  • License Classification: HVAC/R Unlimited Journeyman
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Examination Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Multiple-choice
  • Number of Questions: 70
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes (3 hours)
  • Minimum Passing Score: 70%
  • Examination Type: Open book

State Requirements

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates mechanical licensing and establishes qualifications for HVACR journeymen and contractors.

Minimum Age: Journeyman applicants must be at least 18 years old.

Qualifying Experience: The standard pathway requires three years of verifiable mechanical trade experience.

Education Alternatives: Oklahoma recognizes qualifying approved mechanical education as a substitute for part of the required field experience.

Apprentice Registration: Experience obtained through mechanical apprenticeship activities must satisfy applicable registration requirements.

Journeyman Examination: Candidates must pass the applicable technical examination.

Examination Provider: PSI administers the authorized licensing examination.

Unlimited Classification: The classification covers qualifying heating, cooling, refrigeration, sheet metal, natural gas piping, and related mechanical work.

Code Compliance: Mechanical installations must follow applicable Oklahoma codes, amendments, and construction regulations.

Professional Responsibilities: Journeymen must perform regulated mechanical work within the scope and supervision requirements established by Oklahoma law.

Workplace Safety: HVACR professionals must observe relevant safety requirements during equipment installation, servicing, and repair.

Understanding these requirements helps candidates organize their licensing preparation and professional development.
