Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Journeyman Unlimited examination evaluates technical knowledge related to installing, altering, modifying, servicing, maintaining, and repairing HVACR systems within the scope of the Unlimited Journeyman classification.

Exam preparation should cover the major areas of HVACR work, including Electrical Knowledge and Controls, Natural Gas, Air Distribution Systems, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems, Heating Systems, and Safety.

Electrical Knowledge and Controls can include general electrical theory, motors, AC and DC circuits, and controls. HVACR systems rely heavily on electrical components for equipment operation, system sequencing, safety controls, and troubleshooting, making electrical knowledge an important part of journeyman-level preparation.

Natural Gas preparation should include piping, valves, connectors, materials, hangers and supports, sizing, testing, combustion air, appliances, and venting. Candidates should become familiar with how the International Fuel Gas Code organizes these requirements and practice locating information efficiently.

Air Distribution Systems can include duct sizing and design, duct assembly, materials, installation, supports, insulation, ventilation, exhaust systems, testing and balancing, fire and smoke provisions, and interpretation of plans and symbols. Understanding both practical ductwork concepts and applicable mechanical code requirements can strengthen preparation in this area.

Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems can include refrigeration theory, refrigerants, equipment sizing, system components, piping, equipment installation, operation, maintenance, service, and troubleshooting. Heating preparation can include combustion air, heating equipment, vents and chimneys, equipment sizing, hydronic systems, installation, operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

Safety preparation should include construction-industry requirements relevant to HVACR work. Subjects may include personal protective equipment, workplace hazards, fall protection, ladders, scaffolds, employee protection, and other OSHA-related construction safety requirements.

Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before testing. Permanent tabs may be used, while temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not allowed. Candidates should prepare their references in accordance with the current examination rules before arriving at the testing center.

Open-book testing does not replace technical knowledge. Candidates should be able to recognize the subject of a question before deciding whether a book lookup is necessary. Searching several references for every answer can consume valuable examination time.

A mechanical equipment, ventilation, ductwork, combustion air, or installation question may direct you toward the International Mechanical Code. A fuel gas piping, sizing, material, appliance, combustion-air, testing, or venting question may require the International Fuel Gas Code.

Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning can support broader technical questions involving refrigeration principles, heating, air conditioning, electrical concepts, controls, system components, operation, service, installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

State Requirements

The Oklahoma HVACR Unlimited Journeyman classification authorizes qualifying work involving cooling products, systems, and equipment, including process piping, without the 25-ton limitation that applies to the HVACR Limited Journeyman category.

The Unlimited classification also covers qualifying heating equipment, including process piping, without the 500,000 Btu/h per-appliance limitation that applies to the Limited classification.

The HVACR Unlimited Journeyman scope also includes qualifying sheet metal, natural gas piping, and refrigeration work.

Oklahoma mechanical journeyman applicants must satisfy the applicable experience and licensing requirements for the category they are pursuing. The Unlimited classification requires qualifying experience appropriate to work beyond the capacity limits of the Limited HVACR category.

A licensed Oklahoma mechanical journeyman works within the scope of the journeyman classification and under the applicable contractor structure established by state mechanical licensing requirements. Understanding the difference between Limited and Unlimited HVACR licensing is an important part of choosing the correct license path.
