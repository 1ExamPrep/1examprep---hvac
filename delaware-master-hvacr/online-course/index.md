### Licensing Steps

Begin by reviewing the Delaware Master HVACR license classification and confirming that the unrestricted license matches the HVACR services you plan to provide. Delaware also offers restricted HVACR licenses for designated specialties.

Create an account through Delaware Professional Regulation Online Services and begin the Master HVACR application in DELPROS. Professional licensing applications and supporting documents are submitted through the state’s online system.

Gather the records required for the applicable licensing pathway. These may include license verifications, employment records, apprenticeship documentation, certification records, and other supporting information requested by the Delaware Board.

Applicants for unrestricted Master HVACR licensure must provide the required certification and qualifying experience documentation. The specific application pathway depends on the applicant’s apprenticeship background, work history, and current licenses.

An applicant with an acceptable journeyman certificate may need to provide the apprenticeship program Certificate of Completion and documentation of qualifying work experience obtained after receiving that certificate.

An applicant without an acceptable journeyman certificate may need to document a longer period of qualifying work experience under the supervision of a master licensee. The Board determines whether an Apprenticeship By-Pass Examination is required.

Submit the completed application and all required attachments through DELPROS. The Delaware Board reviews the application before authorizing a candidate to sit for the Master HVACR examination.

After receiving approval, follow the testing provider’s registration and scheduling instructions. Review appointment procedures, identification rules, approved references, calculator requirements, and testing policies before the examination date.

Use the online course and listed references to prepare. Complete technical review, code-navigation exercises, load calculations, duct-sizing practice, equipment study, and timed exam activities.

Complete the examination and earn the required passing score. The official examination result must be accepted through the Delaware licensing process.

Finish any remaining application requirements after passing. Examination success is one part of the licensing process and does not automatically issue the professional license when documents or other requirements remain outstanding.

### State Requirements

A Delaware Master HVACR license permits the license holder to perform services involving heating, air conditioning, refrigeration, ventilation, process cooling, process heating, and related gas-piping systems within the scope established by the state.

A Master HVACR Restricted license allows work only within the specialty identified on the license. Restricted areas include heating involving forced-air systems, ventilation and gas piping; hydronic heating and gas piping; commercial hood systems; refrigeration; air conditioning; and gas piping.

Professionals planning to offer services in more than one restricted specialty should pursue the unrestricted Master HVACR classification. Applicants should select the license that accurately reflects their intended work.

A Master HVACR license does not automatically provide authority for plumbing work involving systems that bring water into a building or remove liquid and water-carried waste. Separate plumbing authority may be required for that work.

Applicants should provide complete, accurate, and consistent documentation. Missing employment verification, incomplete certification records, or conflicting experience information may delay the Board’s review.

The Delaware Board determines whether an applicant qualifies for examination and licensure. The testing provider administers the examination but does not decide whether an applicant meets the state’s professional licensing requirements.

After passing the examination, candidates must complete all remaining requirements before the license can be issued. Applicants should monitor their DELPROS account and email for status updates or requests for additional information.

Licensed Delaware HVACR professionals must comply with applicable renewal, continuing education, professional conduct, and other requirements associated with their licensing period.

A professional HVACR license is separate from a business license. Individuals planning to operate an HVACR contracting business may also need a legal business entity, insurance, tax registration, business licensing, and other operational approvals.
