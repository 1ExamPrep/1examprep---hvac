How does the Master HVACR Contractor exam work?

The Master HVACR Contractor license comes from the Delaware Board of Plumbing, Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Examiners. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  • Delaware issues a Master HVACR license plus restricted master licenses for air conditioning, refrigeration, forced air, hydronic heating and gas piping.
  • A restricted license covers a narrower scope of work than the full Master HVACR license, with its own exam.

Is the Master HVACR Contractor exam open book?

Many HVAC trade exams are open book, but the rule depends on the exam. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed; if books are allowed, highlighted and tabbed sets save the most time.

Who issues the Master HVACR Contractor license in Delaware?

It comes from the Delaware Board of Plumbing, Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Examiners. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as the Master HVACR Contractor license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most working HVAC contractors hold both.
