State Requirements

The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors regulates contractor licensing within the state. The CMC-C classification applies to HVAC/Refrigeration contracting, and applicants must complete the requirements associated with the requested contractor license.

Tennessee requires new contractor license applicants to pass the Tennessee Business and Law examination. A trade examination is also required for applicable classifications, including CMC-C HVAC/Refrigeration.

The licensing process also involves a qualifying agent. The qualifying agent is associated with satisfying the applicable examination requirements for the contracting entity. Applicants should make sure the correct qualifying agent and classification are identified throughout the process.

Financial information is also part of Tennessee contractor licensing. Applicants should prepare the financial and business documentation required for the license application and requested monetary limit.

A contractor license is generally required in Tennessee when a contractor bids or contracts for work at or above the state's applicable contractor licensing threshold. The CMC-C HVAC classification is used for HVAC contracting work requiring the state contractor license.

Passing the CMC-C examination does not automatically issue the contractor license. Examination results form part of the licensing process, and the applicant must complete the remaining requirements established by the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors.
