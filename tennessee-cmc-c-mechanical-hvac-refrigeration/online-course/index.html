Open Book Test

Open-book testing does not mean that studying is unnecessary. HVAC textbooks, mechanical codes, and construction-safety regulations can contain extensive technical information. Candidates who are unfamiliar with the references may spend too much time searching for individual topics.

A strong preparation strategy includes learning both the trade material and the structure of the books. Start by understanding what each reference covers. This makes it easier to narrow your search before opening a book.
