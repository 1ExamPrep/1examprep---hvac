### Is the Commercial Air Conditioning Contractor exam open book?

Many HVAC trade exams are open book, but the rule depends on the exam. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed; if books are allowed, highlighted and tabbed sets save the most time.

### Who issues the Commercial Air Conditioning Contractor license in South Carolina?

It comes from the South Carolina Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation (LLR). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as the Commercial Air Conditioning Contractor license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most working HVAC contractors hold both.
