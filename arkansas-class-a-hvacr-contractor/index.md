## How does the Class A HVACR Contractor exam work?

The Class A HVACR Contractor license comes from the Arkansas HVACR Licensing Board (Department of Labor and Licensing). Prov gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Arkansas HVACR licenses run from Class A to Class E; Class A has no limit on BTUH or horsepower.
- Prov proctors the Arkansas HVACR exams, and the board must approve you before you can sit for one.
- Arkansas has no reciprocal HVACR licensing agreements with other states.

### Is the Class A HVACR Contractor exam open book?

Many HVAC trade exams are open book, but the rule depends on the exam. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed; if books are allowed, highlighted and tabbed sets save the most time.

### Who issues the Class A HVACR Contractor license in Arkansas?

It comes from the Arkansas HVACR Licensing Board (Department of Labor and Licensing). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as the Class A HVACR Contractor license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most working HVAC contractors hold both.
