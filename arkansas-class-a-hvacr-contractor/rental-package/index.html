The International Fuel Gas Code, 2021 supports preparation involving fuel-gas piping, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, installation requirements, inspection, testing, and related safety provisions. The National Electrical Code, 2020 supports preparation involving electrical requirements associated with HVACR systems. Candidates should practice locating information concerning conductors, motors, branch circuits, disconnecting means, overcurrent protection, grounding, controls, equipment, and related wiring requirements.

The NASCLA Arkansas business book supports preparation involving contractor business responsibilities. Subjects include contracts, estimating, project management, financial practices, insurance, bonding, employment matters, taxes, liens, safety administration, and Arkansas contractor business topics.
