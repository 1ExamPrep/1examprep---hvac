### State Requirements

Arkansas HVACR contractor licensing is administered by the Arkansas Department of Labor and Licensing through the State HVAC/R Licensing Board. The Board classifies licenses, establishes licensing rules, oversees examinations, and regulates HVACR work performed under Arkansas law.

A Class A license authorizes HVACR work without limitation to BTUH or horsepower capacity. Because this classification has a broad scope, applicants should be prepared to demonstrate experience and knowledge appropriate for heating, ventilation, air-conditioning, and refrigeration systems.

Applicants must use the current HVACR license application and provide the information required for processing. Incomplete applications or missing supporting documents can delay the licensing process.

Arkansas does not currently maintain reciprocal HVACR licensing agreements with other state HVACR licensing boards. An applicant licensed in another jurisdiction may still need to complete the Arkansas application and examination requirements applicable to the requested classification.

Licensed HVACR contractors must comply with Arkansas HVACR law, Board rules, adopted mechanical and fuel-gas codes, permit requirements, inspection requirements, and applicable professional standards. License holders are also responsible for maintaining their credentials and completing required renewal obligations.

Arkansas HVACR license renewal includes continuing-education responsibilities. Licensed professionals should maintain records and follow the current Board requirements for approved instruction, including applicable mechanical-code education.

The contractor compliance guidance included with The 1 Package helps customers understand how licensing, business formation, tax identification, and continuing operational responsibilities fit together. Final decisions concerning examination eligibility and licensing approval remain with the Arkansas HVAC/R Licensing Board.
