Open Book Test

The Arkansas Class A HVACR Contractor exam is open book. Candidates may use approved references during the examination, subject to the current testing rules concerning permitted editions, highlighting, annotations, permanent tabs, bindings, and loose materials.

An open-book examination still requires serious preparation. Eight technical books contain a substantial amount of information, and candidates may not have enough time to search every reference from the beginning for each question. Effective performance depends on identifying the subject, selecting the correct book, locating the relevant section, and confirming the answer efficiently.

A question involving Arkansas HVACR licensing requirements may direct the candidate to Arkansas HVAC/R Law or Arkansas HVAC/R Rules and Regulations. Fuel-gas piping, venting, appliance, combustion-air, or installation questions may require the International Fuel Gas Code.

Questions involving mechanical equipment, ventilation, ducts, exhaust, combustion air, or refrigeration-code requirements may require the International Mechanical Code. Residential heating and cooling load questions may direct the candidate to Manual J.

Questions involving refrigeration cycles, compressors, condensers, evaporators, metering devices, airflow, controls, service procedures, maintenance, system operation, or troubleshooting may require one of the two refrigeration and air-conditioning textbooks.

Electrical questions involving motors, conductors, branch circuits, disconnecting means, overcurrent protection, grounding, controls, or HVACR equipment may require the National Electrical Code.
