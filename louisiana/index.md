## How does HVAC licensing work in Louisiana?

The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC) licenses HVAC work here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The LSLBC licenses mechanical work, including heat, air conditioning, ventilation, duct work and refrigeration.
- New Orleans also tests stationary engineers in air conditioning and refrigeration with ICC exams 635, 636, 639, 640 and 645.
