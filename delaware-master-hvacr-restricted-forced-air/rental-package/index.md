### Exam Details

The Delaware Master HVACR Restricted Forced Air Systems, Ventilation & Gas Piping examination evaluates the knowledge needed to perform work within this restricted HVACR specialty. The examination contains 70 questions and allows three hours for completion. The published examination outline divides the questions among the following subject areas:

- General Knowledge
- Piping
- Systems and Sizing
- Equipment
- Maintenance
- Plan Reading

Systems and sizing represent a major part of the examination. Candidates should be comfortable reviewing airflow, duct design, system capacity, equipment selection concepts, fuel gas demand, pipe sizing, ventilation requirements, combustion air, and installation conditions. Candidates may also encounter questions that require knowledge of how forced-air systems operate and how individual components work together.

Piping questions can involve fuel gas piping materials, joining methods, supports, protection, valves, regulators, appliance connections, testing, and sizing. A candidate may need to identify the correct code chapter, table, or installation requirement before selecting an answer.

Equipment questions may involve furnaces, burners, blowers, controls, safety devices, air-distribution components, and related mechanical equipment. Candidates should understand equipment operation as well as common installation and service considerations.

Maintenance questions may address inspection procedures, troubleshooting, cleaning, adjustment, system performance, airflow restrictions, burner operation, component condition, and safe servicing practices. Experienced technicians should still review terminology and procedures because exam questions may describe familiar field situations in a technical or code-based format.

Plan-reading questions may require candidates to interpret mechanical drawings, equipment locations, piping routes, duct layouts, symbols, dimensions, elevations, and system arrangements. Carefully review the entire drawing before focusing on one component or measurement.

### State Requirements

Delaware issues an unrestricted Master HVACR license and multiple Master HVACR Restricted licenses. A restricted license authorizes work only within the specialty identified on the license. The Forced Air Systems, Ventilation & Gas Piping classification does not automatically authorize every type of HVACR work covered by an unrestricted license.

The licensing pathway depends on the applicant’s apprenticeship background, journeyperson certificate, supervised experience, and other qualifications. Applicants with an acceptable journeyperson certificate issued after completion of a qualifying HVACR apprenticeship must provide proof of the apprenticeship and at least two years of applicable work experience under the supervision of a master licensee after receiving the certificate.

Applicants who do not have an acceptable journeyperson certificate must provide proof of at least seven years of work experience under the supervision of a master licensee. After the Board reviews and approves the experience, the applicant may also be required to pass the applicable apprenticeship bypass examination before receiving approval for the master exam.

Employment experience is generally documented through verification completed by the appropriate supervisor. Alternative records may be considered when a standard verification cannot be obtained, but acceptance of experience documentation remains subject to Board review.

The Delaware Board must approve applicants before they may sit for the master examination. Applications are submitted through DELPROS, and the Board reviews complete applications with the required supporting documentation.

A professional HVACR license is separate from a Delaware business license. A person who plans to operate a contracting company may also need business registration, tax accounts, insurance, permits, local approvals, and other credentials. This rental package supports examination preparation but does not include business formation or contractor compliance services.
