How does the HVAC Contractor exam work?

The HVAC Contractor license comes from the West Virginia Contractor Licensing Board and Division of Labor. Prov gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  • West Virginia requires an HVAC technician certification from the Division of Labor for HVAC work.
  • Contractor licenses come from the West Virginia Contractor Licensing Board, and Prov gives the exams.
