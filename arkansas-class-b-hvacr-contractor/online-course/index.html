Open Book Test

The Arkansas Class B HVACR Contractor exam is open book. Candidates may use approved references during the examination, subject to the current testing rules concerning permitted editions, highlighting, annotations, permanent tabs, bindings, and loose materials.

An open-book examination still requires serious preparation. Eight technical references contain a substantial amount of information, and candidates may not have enough time to search every book from the beginning for each question. Efficient performance depends on recognizing the subject, selecting the correct reference, locating the applicable section, and confirming the answer without unnecessary searching.

A question involving Arkansas HVACR licensing requirements may direct the candidate to Arkansas HVAC/R Law or Arkansas HVAC/R Rules and Regulations. Fuel-gas piping, appliance, combustion-air, venting, or installation questions may require the International Fuel Gas Code.

Questions involving mechanical equipment, ventilation, duct systems, exhaust, combustion air, or refrigeration-code requirements may require the International Mechanical Code. Residential heating and cooling load questions may direct the candidate to Manual J.
