How do you get an HVAC license?

Who issues HVAC licenses?

Each state sets its own rules. Most license HVAC work through a contractor or mechanical licensing board, such as the Arizona Registrar of Contractors or the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. A few states, including Missouri, Nebraska and Wyoming, leave HVAC licensing to cities. Each state page on this site names the board.

Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as a state license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate. Most working HVAC contractors hold both.

What is the difference between a journeyman and a contractor license?

A journeyman or technician license lets you do the work under a licensed contractor. A contractor license lets you run the business and pull permits, and it usually needs more experience, a business and law exam, insurance and sometimes a bond.

Do many states have a business and law exam?

Many do. Arizona and California applicants, for example, prepare for a business and law exam as well as the trade exam. Packages that include it say so on the product page.

Can I use my license in another state?

Only where the two states have a reciprocity agreement, and many do not. Arkansas, for example, has no reciprocal HVACR licensing agreements with other states. Ask the board in the state where you want to work.

Does buying a package make me eligible to test?

No. Your board or city approves your application first, then the testing provider schedules the exam. A package gets you ready for the test itself.

How does the HVAC exam work?

Is the HVAC contractor exam open book?

Many state trade exams are. The Oklahoma HVACR Unlimited and Arizona C-39 exams are open book, and several Nevada, Connecticut and Idaho exams allow approved books. Your candidate bulletin lists the references and editions allowed.

Who gives the exams?

It depends on the state. PSI, Pearson VUE, Prov and ICC all give HVAC licensing exams. Each state page names the provider where it is confirmed.

How many questions are on the exam?

It varies by state and license. The Oklahoma HVACR Unlimited trade exam, for example, has 90 questions in 240 minutes with a 70 percent passing score. Your candidate bulletin has the exact figures.

Which code books are on the exam?

Most HVAC trade exams draw on the International Mechanical Code, the International Fuel Gas Code, a refrigeration and air conditioning text, ACCA manuals for load and duct design, and OSHA construction safety. The edition depends on your state.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Licensing exam questions are confidential. The courses and practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

How should you study for it?

How long should I study?

Most candidates who work in the trade plan six to eight weeks at about an hour a day: learning the references, practicing calculations and taking timed practice exams.
