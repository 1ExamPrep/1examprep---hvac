### Who issues the Heating Group 1 Class 1 Contractor license in North Carolina?

It comes from the North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as the Heating Group 1 Class 1 Contractor license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most working HVAC contractors hold both.
