Exam Details

The Idaho HVAC Specialty Journeyman exam is part of the state licensing process administered through the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses. Candidates must follow the state application and exam process for the specialty category they are pursuing. The exact exam depends on the specialty license classification selected by the applicant.

Idaho’s HVAC exam materials identify multiple specialty journeyman examinations, including HVAC Hearth Specialty Journeyman, HVAC Waste Oil Specialty Journeyman, and HVAC Fuel Gas Piping Specialty Journeyman. The exam time limit depends on the specialty. The HVAC Hearth Specialty Journeyman exam is listed as a 3-hour exam. The HVAC Waste Oil Specialty Journeyman exam and the HVAC Fuel Gas Piping Specialty Journeyman exam are listed as 2-hour exams.

The minimum passing score for Idaho HVAC specialty journeyman exams is 70%. Idaho’s HVAC exam bulletin identifies the HVAC exam fee as $75, and the fee is non-refundable. Exams are computer-based, and candidates are expected to follow all testing rules, including check-in procedures, identification requirements, reference-book rules, and testing-room conduct.

The exam format includes four-option multiple-choice questions, and Idaho’s HVAC exam bulletin also identifies True/False questions. Because the test is based on approved references and specialty-level code knowledge, candidates should prepare by studying the code book directly and practicing how to locate answers efficiently. The exam should be approached as both a knowledge test and a reference-navigation test.

Open Book Test

The Idaho HVAC specialty journeyman exams are open book. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved reference materials to the testing site. Code books and reference books are not provided at the exam location, so students should prepare in advance with the correct reference and understand how to use it under timed conditions.

Open book does not mean the exam is easy. It means the candidate may use approved references, but the student must still know where information is located and how to apply it correctly. A student who has not practiced with the book may spend too much time searching and not enough time answering. Strong preparation includes learning the table of contents, definitions, chapter layout, key tables, major requirements, and important exceptions.

A practical open-book strategy is to use a “find and confirm” method. First, identify the subject of the question. Next, decide which part of the book is most likely to contain the answer. Then use the reference to confirm the exact requirement before selecting an answer. This method helps students avoid relying only on memory or jobsite habits, especially when a question depends on code wording.

Students may prepare approved references according to Idaho testing rules. Highlighting, tabs, and notes may be allowed when prepared within the exam requirements. Loose paper is not allowed in the reference material. Electronic devices, phones, unauthorized notes, and unapproved materials are not allowed in the testing room. The book should be organized clearly, cleanly, and in a way that supports quick navigation.

Licensing Steps

The Idaho HVAC Specialty Journeyman licensing process begins with selecting the specialty category that matches the work the candidate intends to perform. Applicants should review the state’s specialty journeyman scope before applying, because this classification is more limited than a full HVAC journeyman license. Selecting the correct path helps keep the application, study plan, and exam preparation aligned.

Idaho’s specialty journeyman application materials identify both an education pathway and an examination pathway. The education pathway includes completion of 60 hours of education in fuel gas code and piping installation methods, along with work verification showing 2,000 hours of supervised specialty HVAC installation work. The examination pathway includes passing the board-approved examination after application approval, along with work verification showing 2,000 hours of supervised specialty HVAC installation work.

After the application is submitted and approved, the candidate receives examination information and can continue with scheduling. Once the exam is scheduled, the candidate should prepare with the approved reference material, review specialty-specific topics, and make sure the book is prepared according to testing rules.

A practical licensing plan includes reviewing eligibility, selecting the correct specialty, preparing the application, gathering work verification, receiving exam approval, scheduling the exam, studying with the International Fuel Gas Code, 2018, taking the test, and completing any remaining licensing requirements. State Requirements

Idaho HVAC specialty journeyman applicants must meet the requirements established by the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses and the Idaho Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning Board. The specialty journeyman license is limited in scope and should not be confused with a full Idaho HVAC journeyman license.

Specialty journeyman work may include hearth appliances, non-duct connected oil furnaces, associated fuel gas piping, and venting dedicated exclusively to that specialty work. This license classification does not include plumbing, electrical, or duct work. Candidates should study and apply under the specialty category that matches the work they are qualified to perform.

The state’s application materials identify supervised specialty HVAC installation work as a key requirement. The education pathway includes 60 hours of education in fuel gas code and piping installation methods plus 2,000 hours of supervised specialty HVAC installation work. The examination pathway includes passing the board-approved examination after application approval and verifying 2,000 hours of supervised specialty HVAC installation work.
