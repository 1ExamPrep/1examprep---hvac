### Open Book Test

The Idaho HVAC specialty journeyman exams are open book. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved reference materials to the testing site. Code books and reference books are not provided at the exam location, so students should prepare in advance with the correct reference and understand how to use it under timed conditions.

Open book does not mean the exam is easy. It means the candidate may use approved references, but the student must still know where information is located and how to apply it correctly. A student who has not practiced with the book may spend too much time searching and not enough time answering. Strong preparation includes learning the table of contents, definitions, chapter layout, key tables, major requirements, and important exceptions.

A practical open-book strategy is to use a “find and confirm” method. First, identify the subject of the question. Next, decide which part of the book is most likely to contain the answer. Then use the reference to confirm the exact requirement before selecting an answer. This method helps students avoid relying only on memory or jobsite habits, especially when a question depends on code wording.

Students may prepare approved references according to Idaho testing rules. Highlighting, tabs, and notes may be allowed when prepared within the exam requirements. Loose paper is not allowed in the reference material. Electronic devices, phones, unauthorized notes, and unapproved materials are not allowed in the testing room. The book should be organized clearly, cleanly, and in a way that supports quick navigation.
