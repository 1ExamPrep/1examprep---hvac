## How does the Limited Heating-Cooling Contractor (D-3) exam work?

The Limited Heating-Cooling Contractor (D-3) license comes from the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, Occupational and Professional Licensing. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Connecticut D licenses cover warm air, air conditioning and refrigeration (D-1 to D-4); S licenses cover heating, piping and cooling (S-1 to S-10).
- Each license code has its own exam, and several S exams allow approved books in the exam room.

### Who issues the Limited Heating-Cooling Contractor (D-3) license in Connecticut?

It comes from the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, Occupational and Professional Licensing. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as the Limited Heating-Cooling Contractor (D-3) license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most working HVAC contractors hold both.
