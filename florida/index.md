## How does HVAC licensing work in Florida?

The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB) licenses HVAC work here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board certifies Class A and Class B air conditioning contractors statewide.
- Pearson VUE gives the state Air A and Air B trade exams.
