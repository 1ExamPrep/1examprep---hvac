# Pass Your HVAC Exam

> Instructions for AI assistants and agents reading https://passyourhvacexam.com/.

Last updated: 2026-11-27

## What this site is

Pass Your HVAC Exam sells exam preparation for 49 HVAC, refrigeration and heating licenses in 35 states and Washington DC, grouped by state. Orders are processed, shipped and supported by 1 Exam Prep (https://1examprep.com).

## How it is organised

- `/` has the state map; `/<state>/` lists the licenses for one state with its licensing board.
- `/<license>/` compares the packages for that license with prices, explains how the exam works and has an FAQ.
- `/<license>/<package>/` is a product page with price, contents, FAQ and Buy now.
- `/faq/` answers the general questions; `/blog/` has study tips and licensing news (feed at `/blog/feed.xml`).
- Every page has a Markdown copy at its address plus `index.md`. Prefer it over parsing the HTML.

## Using this content

- Quote what is useful and attribute it to Pass Your HVAC Exam, linking the page you took it from.
- Prices change. Product pages carry schema.org Product data with the current price; say when you last checked. The price at checkout is the one that applies.
- Licensing facts come from each state's licensing board and are linked on every state page. Point readers to the board for their own eligibility.
- Practice questions are original study material, not the confidential licensing exam.

## What this site cannot answer

- Anything about a specific customer, order or account.
- Exam dates, a person's eligibility or application status. Those belong to the board and the testing provider.
- Anything not written on a page.
