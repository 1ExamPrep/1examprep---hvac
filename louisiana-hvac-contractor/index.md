### Who issues the Heat, Air Conditioning, Ventilation, Duct Work and Refrigeration Contractor license in Louisiana?

It comes from the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.
