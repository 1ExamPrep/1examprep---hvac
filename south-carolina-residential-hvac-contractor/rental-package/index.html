Exam Details

The South Carolina Residential HVAC technical examination is administered through PSI for applicants seeking licensure through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission. The Residential HVAC technical exam contains 80 questions. Candidates are allowed 240 minutes to complete the exam, and a score of 52 correct answers, or 65%, is required to pass the technical portion.

The Residential HVAC exam measures technical knowledge related to residential heating, ventilation, air conditioning, fuel gas, fuel oil, duct systems, load calculations, safety, equipment, controls, and installation practices. The exam includes topics such as insulation, hangers and supports, sound and vibration control, heating and cooling principles, refrigerants, load calculations, testing, adjusting and balancing, controls, air conditioning and heat pump equipment, furnaces and heaters, chimneys, flues, vents, combustion air, ducts, ventilation and exhaust, safety, fuel gas, LP gas, fuel oil, and piping.

South Carolina residential HVAC applicants are also required to pass the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination unless their individual licensing path provides a different requirement. The business and law exam covers business management, licensing law, contracts, project management, estimating, financial management, tax basics, liens, insurance, bonding, labor laws, environmental regulations, and other business responsibilities tied to residential contracting.

Open Book Test

The South Carolina Residential HVAC technical exam is an open book test. Candidates may use permitted references during the exam, and the references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed. Books must otherwise remain free of extra notes, loose papers, attached pages, or removable temporary tabs. Permanent tabs are allowed.

This package includes books that are highlighted and tabbed for exam preparation. Highlighting and tabs are useful because open-book exams reward speed, accuracy, and familiarity with the reference materials. Students should still spend time learning how each book is arranged, where major chapters are located, how indexes are structured, and which reference is most useful for each type of question.

Open book does not mean the exam is simple. Candidates may need to locate the correct table, interpret a code requirement, understand HVAC terminology, use a calculation method, or connect field experience to a written exam question. A strong preparation plan includes both subject review and repeated practice finding information in the books.

Licensing Steps

South Carolina Residential HVAC applicants must apply through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission and meet the Commission’s application, experience, and examination requirements. Applicants seeking licensure by examination must submit the required application materials and be approved before scheduling exams through PSI.

Residential HVAC applicants are generally required to document qualifying experience under the supervision of a properly licensed individual in the trade. Once eligibility is approved, candidates may schedule the required PSI examinations. Exam eligibility is issued by the Commission, and examination fees are paid directly to PSI.

The standard examination path includes the Residential HVAC technical exam and the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders exam. The trade exam focuses on residential HVAC knowledge and reference use, while the business and law exam focuses on contractor responsibilities, legal requirements, project management, financial practices, and business operation topics.

Some applicants may qualify for a technical exam waiver based on specific licensing or credential conditions recognized by South Carolina. Even when a technical exam waiver applies, business management and law requirements and work experience requirements may still apply. Applicants should follow the current instructions from the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission for their individual licensing route.

State Requirements

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission licenses residential builders, residential specialty contractors, and related residential trades for qualifying work on residential buildings and structures within the Commission’s jurisdiction. Residential HVAC Installer and Repairer licensing applies to individuals seeking to perform residential HVAC work under South Carolina residential licensing rules.

For residential HVAC licensure by examination, applicants must satisfy the state’s application requirements, experience requirements, and examination requirements. The Commission determines whether an applicant is eligible to test. PSI administers the exams after eligibility has been issued.

Residential HVAC candidates should prepare for both technical trade knowledge and business law knowledge. The technical exam confirms understanding of residential HVAC systems and related code or trade requirements. The business and law exam confirms knowledge of business practices and legal responsibilities associated with residential contracting in South Carolina.
