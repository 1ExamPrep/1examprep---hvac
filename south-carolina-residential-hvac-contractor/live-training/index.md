## How does the Residential HVAC Contractor exam work?

The Residential HVAC Contractor license comes from the South Carolina Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation (LLR). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.
