### Exam Details

The South Carolina Residential HVAC examination is administered as part of the residential specialty licensing process through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission and PSI. The Residential HVAC technical examination contains 80 questions. Candidates need 52 correct answers, or 65%, to pass the technical portion. The time allowed for the Residential HVAC technical exam is 240 minutes.

The Residential HVAC exam covers residential HVAC work, including air conditioning and heat pump equipment, LP and natural gas, related piping, fuel oil appliances and piping, ductwork, and associated trades. ### Open Book Test

The South Carolina Residential HVAC examination is an open book test. Candidates may bring permitted reference books listed for the examination. Reference books may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed, but they must otherwise be unmarked and may not contain additional papers, loose notes, or attached pages. Permanent tabs are allowed. Temporary tabs, such as removable sticky notes or tabs that can be removed without tearing the page, are not allowed.

Because the test is open book, speed and organization are important. You should know which reference is most useful for each subject area before exam day. ### Licensing Steps

Individuals seeking a South Carolina Residential HVAC Installer and Repairer license by examination must first apply through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission. The Commission reviews the application, required documentation, fees, and experience information before approving an applicant for examination eligibility.

Work experience must be verified and approved before an applicant is made eligible for the exam. Once approved, the applicant receives examination eligibility information and may schedule the required exam with PSI. Examination eligibility is valid for one year. Candidates are allowed three attempts to pass the required examination within a 12-month period.

Applicants pursuing licensure by examination must pass the Residential HVAC technical examination and the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination. The trade exam confirms technical knowledge in residential HVAC work, while the business and law exam focuses on business management, licensing laws, contracts, project management, estimating, financial management, taxes, liens, insurance, bonding, labor laws, environmental rules, and related business topics.

South Carolina also provides certain exam waiver paths for qualified applicants. The Residential HVAC technical portion may be waived for applicants who meet specific criteria, such as holding a qualifying South Carolina Mechanical Contractor license in the required field, holding a qualifying license from an eligible state, or holding an eligible HARV Master Mechanical certification issued by examination under the applicable program dates. Work experience requirements and the Business Management and Law portion still apply when the technical exam is waived.

Applicants should follow the current instructions from the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission when applying, submitting documentation, paying fees, and scheduling exams. The Commission determines eligibility, and PSI administers the examination process.
