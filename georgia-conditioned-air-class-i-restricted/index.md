### Who issues the Conditioned Air Contractor Class I (Restricted) license in Georgia?

It comes from the Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Conditioned Air Contractors. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### Do I need EPA 608 certification as well as the Conditioned Air Contractor Class I (Restricted) license?

Usually, yes, if you handle refrigerant. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification and the state or city license is separate; most working HVAC contractors hold both.
