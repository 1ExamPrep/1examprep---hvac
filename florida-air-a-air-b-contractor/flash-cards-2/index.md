### State Requirements

Florida recognizes different air conditioning contractor classifications based on the scope and capacity of the systems contractors are authorized to work on.

### Florida Class A Air Conditioning Contractor

A Class A Air Conditioning Contractor is authorized to perform a broad range of central air conditioning, heating, ventilation, and refrigeration contracting activities within the statutory scope of the license.

Unlike Class B, the Class A classification is not restricted by the 25-ton cooling and 500,000-BTU heating limitations.

This classification is relevant to contractors seeking broader authority to install, maintain, repair, alter, and design qualifying HVAC systems.

### Florida Class B Air Conditioning Contractor

A Class B Air Conditioning Contractor works within a more limited system-capacity classification. Florida defines the Class B scope as systems involving no more than **25 tons of cooling and 500,000 BTU of heating in any one system**.

Class B contractors may perform qualifying installation, repair, maintenance, alteration, and related HVAC activities within their authorized scope.

Neither classification automatically authorizes work outside its statutory definition, including certain plumbing, fuel-line, and electrical power wiring activities.

### Experience and Education

Florida certified contractor applicants generally must demonstrate qualifying experience or an accepted combination of education and experience. The applicable licensing application establishes the documentation and qualifications required for the selected classification.

Applicants must also satisfy relevant financial responsibility and other licensing conditions.

### Business & Finance Examination Exemptions

Contractors who already hold a current, active license issued by Florida's Construction Industry Licensing Board may qualify for an exemption from the Business & Finance examination when pursuing another applicable license category.

Applicants pursuing their initial certification should prepare for the examination requirements associated with their selected classification.
