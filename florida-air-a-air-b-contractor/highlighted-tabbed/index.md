### State Requirements

Florida regulates air conditioning contracting through state construction licensing laws and the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

**Class A Air Conditioning Contractors** have a broader authorized scope involving central air conditioning, refrigeration, heating, ventilation, ductwork, and related mechanical systems within Florida's statutory requirements.

**Class B Air Conditioning Contractors** operate within specified equipment capacity limits, including 25 tons of cooling and 500,000 Btu of heating in any one system, together with other statutory restrictions.

Both classifications require an understanding of applicable mechanical codes, construction safety, equipment installations, and energy conservation requirements.

Certified contractors must also satisfy applicable licensing, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative requirements.
