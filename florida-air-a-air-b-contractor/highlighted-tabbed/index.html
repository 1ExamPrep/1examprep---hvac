Exam Details

Florida offers separate contractor classifications for Class A Air Conditioning Contractors and Class B Air Conditioning Contractors. Both classifications involve heating, cooling, ventilation, and refrigeration knowledge, but their authorized scopes of work differ.

The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation and Construction Industry Licensing Board oversee state contractor certification requirements.

  • Examination Categories: Florida Class A Air Conditioning and Class B Air Conditioning
  • Licensing Authority: Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
  • Licensing Board: Construction Industry Licensing Board
  • Examination Format: Open-book, multiple-choice testing
  • Core Trade Subjects: Refrigeration, air conditioning, ductwork, ventilation, heating, maintenance, and construction safety
  • Additional Licensing Examination: Business and Finance

The trade examinations address important areas such as pre-installation planning, equipment operation, system components, installation methods, air distribution, energy management, maintenance, and safe work practices.

Successful preparation requires the ability to interpret code provisions, recognize technical requirements, understand HVAC system operation, and navigate several reference publications.

Open Book Test

Florida Class A and Class B Air Conditioning Contractor trade examinations are open-book tests. Candidates may bring reference materials specifically authorized for their examination category and testing period.

Because HVAC examinations involve multiple books and standards, reference navigation is an important study skill. Candidates should know which publication contains information about a particular system, calculation, installation method, or safety requirement.

Highlighted and tabbed books support preparation by making it easier to locate important subjects and become familiar with the organization of reference materials.

Florida examination rules permit highlighting and underlining in approved references. Permanently attached tabs containing normal chapter or page headings are permitted, while removable tabs, handwritten notes, and unauthorized inserted materials are prohibited.

The Florida licensing authority publishes separate reference lists for Air A and Air B examinations, including lists for different testing periods. Only references authorized for the scheduled examination may enter the testing room.

Reviewing marked sections before examination day can strengthen familiarity with technical tables, installation requirements, definitions, and important publication headings.

State Requirements

Florida regulates air conditioning contracting through state construction licensing laws and the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

Class A Air Conditioning Contractors have a broader authorized scope involving central air conditioning, refrigeration, heating, ventilation, ductwork, and related mechanical systems within Florida's statutory requirements.

Class B Air Conditioning Contractors operate within specified equipment capacity limits, including 25 tons of cooling and 500,000 Btu of heating in any one system, together with other statutory restrictions.

Both classifications require an understanding of applicable mechanical codes, construction safety, equipment installations, and energy conservation requirements.

Certified contractors must also satisfy applicable licensing, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative requirements.
