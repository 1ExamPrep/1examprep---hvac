Exam Details

The Florida Class A Air Conditioning Contractor examination evaluates technical knowledge associated with installing, maintaining, servicing, and repairing air conditioning, refrigeration, heating, ventilation, and related mechanical systems.

The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) and the Construction Industry Licensing Board oversee contractor certification requirements.

  • Examination: Florida Class A Air Conditioning Contractor Trade Knowledge
  • Licensing Authority: Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
  • Licensing Board: Construction Industry Licensing Board
  • Examination Registration: Professional Testing, Inc.
  • Examination Format: Computer-based, multiple-choice testing
  • Reference Format: Open book
  • Additional Licensing Examination: Business and Finance

Major subjects associated with Air A trade preparation include pre-installation planning, sheet metal ductwork, refrigeration and air conditioning systems, heating systems, maintenance, electrical components, energy management, indoor air quality, and construction safety.

Class A Air Conditioning Contractor certification covers a broad range of HVACR work under Florida law. The trade examination reflects the technical knowledge needed to understand different systems, installation methods, and equipment applications.

This product focuses on online trade practice questions. It does not replace the separate Business and Finance examination preparation required for Florida contractor certification.

Open Book Test

The Florida Class A Air Conditioning Contractor trade examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult references specifically approved for the examination category and scheduled testing period.

Open-book examinations require both technical knowledge and efficient reference navigation. Candidates must understand a question, recognize the applicable topic, locate relevant information, and determine the correct answer.

For example, a question about ventilation fire protection may involve an NFPA standard. Questions about duct construction may involve SMACNA publications, while mechanical installation and energy conservation questions may involve the Florida Building Code.

Online practice questions help candidates develop the habit of identifying the correct technical subject before searching through reference materials.

Florida publishes separate examination reference lists for Class A and Class B Air Conditioning Contractors. The reference editions applicable to examinations beginning January 1, 2027, are listed separately from those applicable during 2026.

Study references, including editions used for preparation, are not automatically authorized for examination-room use. The official reference list for the scheduled examination determines which materials may be brought into the testing center.

Practicing with indexes, definitions, calculations, technical tables, diagrams, and installation standards can strengthen the research skills needed for open-book testing.

State Requirements

Florida Class A Air Conditioning Contractor certification is regulated under the state's construction contracting laws and administered through the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

The Class A classification covers a broad range of authorized heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration activities. This includes mechanical systems, associated ductwork, and other work permitted within the statutory scope of the license.

Unlike the Class B classification, Class A Air Conditioning Contractors are not subject to the same 25-ton cooling and 500,000-Btu heating capacity limitations.

Contractors must understand applicable building codes, mechanical installation requirements, equipment standards, construction safety, and licensing responsibilities.

Trade examination preparation emphasizes the technical knowledge underlying these activities, including proper equipment application, airflow, energy efficiency, and code interpretation.

Reference Books

The Florida Air A Contractor Exam Online Practice Questions focus on the following 15 listed technical study references:

  • Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Troubleshooting Handbook, 2nd Edition, 2003
    Provides technical information concerning refrigeration operation, equipment diagnostics, troubleshooting methods, service procedures, and common system problems. Useful for reviewing practical HVACR service concepts.
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA)
    Contains federal construction safety regulations addressing electrical hazards, personal protective equipment, ladders, scaffolding, excavation, fall protection, tools, and workplace conditions.
  • Contractors Manual, 2025
    Supports study of construction contractor operations, management responsibilities, licensing-related subjects, and business administration principles.
  • Pipefitters Handbook, 3rd Edition
    Provides information involving pipe dimensions, fittings, piping layout, measurements, and technical reference data relevant to mechanical and HVAC installations.
  • NFPA 90A Installation of Air Conditioning and Ventilating Systems, 2024
    Addresses ventilation and air conditioning installation standards, duct systems, fire protection, and related mechanical safety requirements.
  • NFPA 90B Installation of Warm Air Heating and Air Conditioning Systems, 2027
    Addresses warm-air heating and air conditioning system installation requirements, including important building and fire safety considerations.
  • NFPA 96, Standard for Ventilation Control and Fire Protection of Commercial Cooking Operations, 2024
    Provides standards involving commercial cooking ventilation, exhaust hoods, duct systems, grease-related fire hazards, and fire protection.
  • SMACNA - Fibrous Glass Duct Construction Standards, 8th Edition, 2021
    Contains construction standards for fibrous glass duct systems, including materials, fabrication, reinforcement, installation, and duct construction requirements.
  • HVAC Duct Construction Standards, Metal and Flexible
    Addresses metal and flexible duct construction, support methods, reinforcement, installation practices, fittings, and air distribution requirements.
  • SMACNA - Energy Systems Analysis and Management, 2nd Edition, 2014
    Supports understanding of building energy performance, system evaluation, energy management principles, and HVAC operational efficiency.
  • Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technology, 10th Edition
    A comprehensive HVACR technical textbook covering refrigeration cycles, refrigerants, compressors, condensers, evaporators, motors, electrical controls, heating, maintenance, and troubleshooting.
  • Ductulator
    A duct-sizing calculation reference used to evaluate airflow, duct dimensions, air velocity, and friction loss in air distribution systems.
  • Energy Efficient Building Construction in Florida, 2024
    Addresses building energy performance, insulation, heating and cooling efficiency, building-envelope characteristics, and energy-efficient construction principles relevant to Florida.
  • Florida Building Code - Mechanical, 2026
    Contains mechanical construction provisions covering heating and cooling equipment, ventilation, duct installations, exhaust systems, and mechanical safety.
  • Florida Building Code - Energy Conservation, 2026
    Addresses energy conservation requirements involving mechanical equipment efficiency, insulation, system controls, building-envelope performance, and related provisions.

Physical copies of these 15 references are not included. The online practice questions emphasize the technical subjects associated with these publications.
