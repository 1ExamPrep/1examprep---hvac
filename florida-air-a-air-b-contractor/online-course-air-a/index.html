Exam Details

The Florida Class A Air Conditioning Contractor Trade Knowledge Examination evaluates a candidate's understanding of refrigeration, HVAC equipment, duct systems, ventilation, maintenance, electrical controls, energy management, and construction safety.

The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation and Construction Industry Licensing Board oversee the certification process. Professional Testing, Inc. handles construction examination registration, and computer-based testing is administered through Pearson VUE.

  • Examination: Florida Class A Air Conditioning Contractor Trade Knowledge
  • Licensing Authority: Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
  • Licensing Board: Construction Industry Licensing Board
  • Examination Registration: Professional Testing, Inc.
  • Testing Provider: Pearson VUE
  • Examination Type: Multiple choice
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Additional Licensing Examination: Business and Finance

Major examination subjects include pre-installation planning, HVAC equipment installation, sheet metal ductwork, refrigeration and air conditioning systems, heating, equipment maintenance, ventilation, indoor air quality, energy management, and safety.

State Requirements

Florida regulates Class A Air Conditioning Contractors through its construction licensing laws and the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

The Class A classification covers a broad range of authorized heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration, ductwork, and related mechanical contracting activities.

Unlike the Class B classification, Class A Air Conditioning Contractors are not subject to the same cooling and heating equipment-capacity limitations.

Applicants must meet applicable experience or education qualifications, pass the required examinations, and satisfy financial responsibility, insurance, background screening, and other licensing requirements.

Contractors are also responsible for understanding applicable mechanical codes, building requirements, construction safety, energy conservation, and permitted contracting activities.

    Addresses building energy performance, insulation, mechanical efficiency, and energy-conscious construction methods.
