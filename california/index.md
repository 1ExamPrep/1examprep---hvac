## How does HVAC licensing work in California?

The Contractors State License Board (CSLB) licenses HVAC work here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The CSLB licenses C-20 warm-air heating, ventilating and air conditioning, C-38 refrigeration and C-4 boiler contractors.
- California applicants take the Law and Business exam as well as the trade exam, so the packages pair both courses.
- One package adds the application, the state fees and the first-year bond.
