### State Requirements

The Maryland Board of Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Contractors establishes the qualifications and professional responsibilities associated with Limited Contractor licensing.

The standard qualification pathway includes:

- **Journeyman Licensing:** Holding a Maryland HVACR journeyman license for at least two years.
- **Qualifying Employment:** Regularly and principally performing HVACR services under the direction and control of a properly licensed contractor.
- **Recent Work Experience:** Completing at least 1,000 hours of qualifying HVACR work during the year preceding the application.
- **Examination:** Passing the Limited HVACR Contractor examination with a score of at least 70%.
- **License Application:** Completing the requirements established by the Maryland HVACR Board.

Maryland law also authorizes the Board to evaluate certain comparable professional education, training, and work experience.

**A Maryland Limited HVACR Contractor license authorizes maintenance and repair, not the installation or retrofit of complete HVACR systems.** The authorized work may involve heating, air conditioning, refrigeration, ventilation, or hydronic systems within the applicable license scope.

Limited contractors performing contracting activities may also be subject to liability insurance and license-number advertising requirements under Maryland law.

Understanding the difference between limited maintenance-and-repair authority and broader contractor classifications is an important part of professional licensing preparation.
