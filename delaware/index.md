## How does HVAC licensing work in Delaware?

The Delaware Board of Plumbing, Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Examiners licenses HVAC work here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Delaware issues a Master HVACR license plus restricted master licenses for air conditioning, refrigeration, forced air, hydronic heating and gas piping.
- A restricted license covers a narrower scope of work than the full Master HVACR license, with its own exam.
