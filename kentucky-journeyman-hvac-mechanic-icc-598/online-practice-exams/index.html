Exam Details

The Kentucky Journeyman HVAC Mechanic examination is administered through the International Code Council (ICC) contractor and trades examination program. It evaluates knowledge of HVAC installation practices, mechanical codes, safety principles, equipment operation, and technical requirements.

  • Exam Name: Kentucky Journeyman HVAC
  • Exam Code: 598-KY
  • Exam Organization: International Code Council (ICC)
  • Testing Provider: Pearson VUE
  • Exam Format: Computer-based, multiple-choice examination
  • Number of Questions: 60
  • Time Limit: 3 hours
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Online Practice Access: 3 months

The ICC examination outline divides the Kentucky Journeyman HVAC examination into five major subject areas:

  • HVAC Principles, Terminology, Administration, and Safety – 23%: Fundamental HVAC concepts, industry terminology, mechanical requirements, and safety practices.
  • HVAC Installation Requirements – 15%: Installation principles, equipment requirements, and relevant mechanical code provisions.
  • Venting, Combustion Air, and Gas Piping – 21%: Fuel gas systems, combustion air requirements, venting principles, and piping-related considerations.
  • Boilers, Duct, Electrical, and Exhaust – 21%: Heating equipment, duct systems, electrical concepts, and mechanical exhaust requirements.
  • Plan Analysis – 20%: Understanding and interpreting information presented in mechanical plans and construction documents.

These examination categories provide a useful framework for organizing your study sessions. Because the test covers several areas of HVAC knowledge, successful preparation requires attention to both technical principles and the application of mechanical code requirements.

Plan analysis, safety, and installation topics are especially important for candidates who are accustomed to hands-on fieldwork but have less experience answering code-based examination questions.

The Kentucky ICC Journeyman HVAC Mechanic Exam (598-KY) is an open book test. Candidates may use approved examination references in accordance with ICC and Pearson VUE testing rules.

Although the examination is open book, understanding where to find information is essential. With 60 questions and a three-hour time limit, candidates benefit from recognizing common mechanical code subjects and navigating reference materials efficiently.

Many HVAC code questions require attention to details such as equipment requirements, installation conditions, clearances, combustion air, venting, or specific system configurations.

Under Kentucky's HVAC licensing framework, journeyman mechanics perform HVAC work under the supervision of a master HVAC contractor. The journeyman classification covers work involving air conditioning, warm-air heating, ventilation, equipment start-up, and associated duct systems.

Reference Books

The following publications provide a foundation for studying mechanical codes, fuel gas systems, and HVAC technical principles.

  • International Mechanical Code, 2015
    The 2015 International Mechanical Code (IMC) establishes provisions for mechanical system installation, equipment, ventilation, exhaust, ducts, and other building mechanical requirements. It helps candidates understand the code-based principles behind safe HVAC installation and operation. This edition is listed among the ICC-approved references for Kentucky Exam 598.
  • NFPA 54 - Standard for National Fuel Gas Code, 2012
    The 2012 National Fuel Gas Code provides technical material relating to fuel gas piping, appliance installation, combustion air, venting, and fuel gas safety. It supports study of important gas-fired equipment and installation concepts. The ICC examination reference list specifies the 2009 edition for exam-room use.
  • Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technology, 10th Edition
    This comprehensive HVAC textbook explains refrigeration fundamentals, heating and cooling systems, electrical controls, equipment components, installation procedures, service practices, and troubleshooting principles. It supports technical study and helps connect theoretical concepts with practical HVAC work. The ICC examination list identifies the 8th or 9th Edition for approved exam references.

Studying with these publications can help candidates strengthen their understanding of both code requirements and the underlying mechanical principles that govern HVAC system performance.

The mechanical code and fuel gas code focus on installation requirements and safety provisions, while the HVAC technology textbook provides broader technical explanations of equipment and system operation.
