### Exam Details

The **Oklahoma HVACR Contractor, Limited Examination** is administered by PSI for the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB). It evaluates knowledge of heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration, electrical controls, natural gas, duct systems, and construction safety.

- **Examination:** Oklahoma HVACR Contractor, Limited
- **Licensing Authority:** Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Examination Format:** Open book
- **Number of Questions:** 80
- **Time Allowed:** 210 minutes
- **Passing Score:** 70%, equivalent to 56 correct answers

The examination is divided into six technical categories:

- **Electrical Knowledge and Controls:** 15 questions
- **Natural Gas:** 12 questions
- **Air Distribution Systems:** 15 questions
- **Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems:** 20 questions
- **Heating Systems:** 15 questions
- **Safety:** 3 questions

Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems is the largest examination category, representing 20 of the 80 questions. Electrical controls, air distribution, and heating systems are also important areas of preparation.

### State Requirements

The **Oklahoma Construction Industries Board** regulates mechanical contractor licensing and establishes requirements for individuals performing covered HVACR work.

An **HVACR Limited Contractor** may perform authorized work involving cooling equipment with a capacity of no more than 25 tons, heating equipment with a heat input of no more than 500,000 BTU per hour per appliance, and other work permitted within the limited mechanical classification.

Mechanical contractor applicants must satisfy the applicable journeyman qualifications and obtain an additional year of qualifying mechanical trade experience.

For an active contractor license, Oklahoma also requires proof of appropriate surety bond and liability insurance coverage. Contractors must maintain applicable licensing and business requirements while performing regulated work.

Business formation and mechanical contractor licensing are separate responsibilities. Establishing an LLC or corporation creates a legal business entity, while the mechanical contractor license authorizes work within the approved classification.
