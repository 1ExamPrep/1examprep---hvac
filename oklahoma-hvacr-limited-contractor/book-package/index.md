### Open Book Test

The Oklahoma HVACR Contractor, Limited examination is an **open book test**. Approved reference materials may be taken into the examination center under PSI testing rules.

PSI allows approved references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are permitted, while temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not allowed. References cannot contain written notes, and candidates may not write in their references during the examination session.

The testing rules also allow a silent, non-printing, non-programmable calculator. This can be useful for HVACR questions involving mathematics, sizing, and other calculations.

An open-book format does not eliminate the need to study. Candidates should know which reference applies to each type of question and practice finding information efficiently. Looking through several books for every answer can consume valuable exam time.

For example, mechanical-system questions may point toward the International Mechanical Code, while natural gas installation or sizing questions may require the International Fuel Gas Code. Residential load calculations may involve Manual J, air-distribution calculations may involve the Ductulator, and construction-safety questions may require 29 CFR Part 1926.

During preparation, practice identifying the subject before opening a reference. Then locate the appropriate chapter, section, table, formula, or index entry. Repeating this process can help build stronger reference-navigation habits for the open-book exam.

### Licensing Steps

Oklahoma mechanical contractor licensing is administered by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The general contractor licensing path includes meeting the required mechanical experience qualifications, submitting the appropriate application, receiving examination eligibility, completing the required examination process, and satisfying the requirements for an active contractor license.

1. **Gain qualifying mechanical experience.** Develop the verifiable trade experience required for Oklahoma mechanical licensing.
2. **Meet contractor-level experience requirements.** Contractor applicants must satisfy the applicable journeyman qualifications and obtain the additional mechanical trade experience required for contractor licensing.
3. **Submit the mechanical contractor application.** Apply through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board for the appropriate contractor classification.
4. **Receive examination eligibility.** The Construction Industries Board reviews the application and determines eligibility to test.
5. **Schedule the contractor examination.** Approved applicants schedule their examination through PSI.
6. **Complete the required contractor examinations.** Successfully complete the applicable technical and contractor licensing examination requirements.
7. **Complete active contractor requirements.** Provide the required documentation for active contractor status after satisfying the examination requirements.

### State Requirements

The Oklahoma HVACR Limited Contractor classification covers qualifying cooling products, systems, and equipment, including related process piping, with a cooling capacity of no more than 25 tons.

The classification also covers heating equipment, including process piping, with a heat input of no more than 500,000 Btu/h per appliance. Within the authorized classification, HVACR Limited licensing also includes refrigeration, sheet metal, natural gas piping, and related process piping.

Applicants seeking mechanical contractor licensing must meet Oklahoma’s applicable contractor qualifications. Contractor applicants must satisfy the requirements applicable to a journeyman and obtain an additional year of experience in the mechanical trade.

A mechanical contractor must also be a permanent employee, owner, partner, or officer of the mechanical firm. Candidates should approach licensing as more than an examination process because meeting experience, application, and active-license requirements is also part of becoming an Oklahoma mechanical contractor.
