Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Contractor, Limited Examination is administered by PSI as part of the licensing examination program for the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.

The examination evaluates knowledge of cooling equipment, heating systems, refrigeration, air distribution, electrical controls, natural gas, and mechanical construction safety.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma HVACR Contractor, Limited
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Number of Questions: 80
  • Time Allowed: 210 minutes
  • Passing Score: 70%, equivalent to 56 correct answers

The examination includes six major technical categories:

  • Electrical Knowledge and Controls: 15 questions
  • Natural Gas: 12 questions
  • Air Distribution Systems: 15 questions
  • Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems: 20 questions
  • Heating Systems: 15 questions
  • Safety: 3 questions

Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems is the largest examination category, representing 20 of the 80 questions. Electrical Knowledge and Controls, Air Distribution Systems, and Heating Systems are also substantial preparation areas.

The Oklahoma mechanical contractor licensing examination process also includes a separate Mechanical Business and Law examination. The package contains Oklahoma statutes, administrative rules, and the NASCLA contractor business guide to support preparation for those subjects.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma HVACR Limited Contractor examination is an open book test. Candidates may consult authorized reference materials during the examination according to PSI examination-room rules.

Open-book testing requires technical knowledge and effective reference navigation. Candidates must understand the questions, select the appropriate code or technical publication, and locate the necessary information within the examination's time limit.

The examination's trade references address mechanical equipment, fuel gas installations, refrigeration systems, air distribution, load calculations, trade mathematics, and construction safety.

State Requirements

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates mechanical contractor licensing and establishes requirements for HVACR professionals performing covered work.

An Oklahoma HVACR Limited Contractor license authorizes work within the state's limited mechanical classification, including cooling systems with capacities of no more than 25 tons and heating equipment with heat inputs of no more than 500,000 BTU per hour per appliance.

The classification also addresses authorized sheet metal, natural gas piping, and refrigeration work.

Mechanical contractor applicants must satisfy applicable journeyman-level qualifications and additional qualifying mechanical trade experience requirements.

Oklahoma mechanical contractor licensing also involves appropriate financial responsibility, insurance, and surety bond requirements for active contractor status.

The state's Mechanical Licensing Act and Mechanical Industry Regulations establish important licensing and professional responsibilities. These publications are included among the package's Business and Law study materials.

Understanding Oklahoma laws and regulations is important for both examination preparation and professional contracting operations.
