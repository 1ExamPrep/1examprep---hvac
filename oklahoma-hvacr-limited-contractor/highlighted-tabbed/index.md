### Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Contractor, Limited examination is administered by PSI for candidates approved through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The exam evaluates technical knowledge associated with installing, maintaining, repairing, fabricating, altering, and extending air conditioning, refrigeration, heating, and ventilation systems within the limited contractor classification.

- **Exam:** Oklahoma HVACR Contractor, Limited
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Number of Questions:** 80
- **Time Allowed:** 210 minutes
- **Passing Score:** 70%
- **Passing Raw Score:** 56 correct answers
- **Exam Format:** Open book

The examination is divided into six major technical areas. Electrical Knowledge and Controls contains 15 questions. Natural Gas contains 12 questions. Air Distribution Systems contains 15 questions. Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems contains 20 questions. Heating Systems contains 15 questions, and Safety contains 3 questions.

Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems is the largest individual section. Topics include refrigeration theory, refrigerants, equipment sizing and design, components, piping, equipment installation, system operation, troubleshooting, and maintenance.

Heating Systems includes heating theory and types, combustion air, vents and chimneys, equipment sizing, heating equipment, installation, system operation, troubleshooting, maintenance, hydronics, and hydronic piping.

Air Distribution Systems includes duct definitions, sizing and design, duct assembly and installation, hangers and supports, duct materials and shapes, insulation, fire and smoke control, hoods and exhaust systems, testing and balancing, ventilation requirements, plans, and symbols.

Natural Gas questions address piping valves, connectors, accessories, hangers and supports, sizing, materials, and testing. Electrical Knowledge and Controls covers general electrical theory, motors, DC circuits, AC circuits, and controls. Safety topics include personal protective equipment, employee safety training, ventilation, first aid, fall protection, lockout/tagout, ladders, scaffolds, and related construction safety subjects.

### Open Book Test

The Oklahoma HVACR Contractor, Limited examination is an **open book test**. Candidates are responsible for bringing approved references to the examination center and must follow PSI rules governing the condition and preparation of those materials.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are permitted. Temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not allowed. Candidates may not write in their references before or during the examination session, and additional loose papers cannot be brought into the test room with the approved books.

This makes preparation with highlighted and tabbed references particularly useful. An open-book exam does not mean every answer can be found quickly without preparation. Candidates still need to identify which reference applies to a question, locate the correct section, and interpret the information within the available testing time.

Mechanical-system questions may direct you toward the International Mechanical Code. Fuel gas questions may require the International Fuel Gas Code. Refrigeration, heating, controls, and equipment questions may involve Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning. Residential load-calculation study can involve Manual J, while air-distribution work can involve the Ductulator. OSHA safety questions may require 29 CFR Part 1926.

### State Requirements

The Oklahoma HVACR Limited Contractor classification establishes specific limits for the size of HVACR equipment that may be covered by the license.

An HVACR Limited Contractor may perform qualifying work involving cooling products, systems, or equipment, including process piping, with a cooling capacity of no more than **25 tons**.

The classification also includes qualifying heating equipment and related process piping with a heat input of no more than **500,000 Btu/h per appliance**.

The HVACR Limited classification also incorporates refrigeration, sheet metal, natural gas, and related process piping within the authorized scope of the license.

Oklahoma mechanical contractor applicants must satisfy the requirements applicable to a journeyman and have an additional year of experience in the mechanical trade. A mechanical contractor must also be a permanent employee, owner, partner, or officer of the mechanical firm.

Licensing involves more than passing the examination. Applicants should complete the applicable Construction Industries Board licensing process and meet the requirements associated with maintaining an active Oklahoma mechanical contractor license.
