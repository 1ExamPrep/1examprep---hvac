Exam Details

The Oklahoma HVACR Limited Contractor Examination evaluates knowledge of mechanical systems and the trade practices associated with heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration contracting.

The licensing process is overseen by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board, with examinations administered through PSI.

  • Examination: Oklahoma HVACR Contractor, Limited
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Number of Questions: 80
  • Time Allowed: 210 minutes
  • Passing Score: 70%

The examination focuses on several technical subjects, including refrigeration and air conditioning, heating systems, electrical knowledge and controls, air distribution systems, natural gas, and safety.

Refrigeration and air conditioning requires familiarity with compressors, refrigerants, system components, installation practices, performance evaluation, and troubleshooting principles.

Electrical knowledge involves controls, motors, electrical measurements, switching components, and equipment operation. Air distribution questions focus on duct systems, airflow, ventilation, and related calculations.

Heating and natural gas subjects address equipment, combustion air, venting, gas piping, and the requirements associated with installing mechanical systems safely.

Oklahoma contractor applicants also prepare for applicable Mechanical Business and Law examination requirements, covering state regulations and general contractor management responsibilities.

State Requirements

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates mechanical contractor licensing and oversees professional requirements associated with covered HVACR work in Oklahoma.

The HVACR Limited Contractor classification addresses certain authorized heating, cooling, refrigeration, ventilation, and mechanical activities within the limits established by Oklahoma law.

The limited classification includes cooling equipment with capacities of no more than 25 tons and heating equipment with heat inputs of no more than 500,000 BTU per hour per appliance.

Applicants must satisfy the applicable professional qualifications and examination requirements before obtaining a contractor license.

Mechanical contractors also have responsibilities involving regulated work, contractor administration, insurance, bonding, and ongoing professional compliance.

Oklahoma's Mechanical Licensing Act and Mechanical Industry Regulations provide important information about licensing obligations and industry responsibilities.
