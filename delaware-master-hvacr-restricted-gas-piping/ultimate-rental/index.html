Exam Details

The Delaware Master HVACR Restricted – Gas Piping examination evaluates knowledge associated with gas piping systems and the safe performance of work within this restricted specialty. Candidates should be prepared to interpret technical questions, apply trade principles, complete calculations, read piping layouts, and locate supporting information in the approved examination references.

Preparation for this examination should include the following knowledge areas:

  • General gas piping knowledge and terminology
  • Fuel gas code organization and navigation
  • Gas piping materials and joining methods
  • System installation requirements
  • Gas pipe sizing and design
  • Valves, regulators, shutoffs, and related components
  • Appliance connections and installation considerations
  • Combustion air and venting principles
  • Liquefied petroleum gas concepts
  • System testing and inspection principles
  • Isometric drawings and plan reading
  • Pipefitting mathematics and measurements
  • Workplace and construction safety

General knowledge questions may involve tools, materials, system components, accepted trade practices, and terminology used in gas piping work. Candidates should understand the purpose of common valves, fittings, regulators, protective devices, and appliance connections, along with the conditions that affect their use.

System installation is an important area of preparation. Questions may require candidates to identify acceptable materials, joining methods, support requirements, protection methods, equipment clearances, shutoff provisions, or testing procedures. Read each question carefully because details such as system type, location, material, appliance, or installation condition can affect the applicable rule.

Gas pipe sizing questions may require the use of tables in the International Fuel Gas Code. Candidates should practice identifying the correct sizing method, selecting the applicable table, interpreting demand and developed length, and following the table accurately. Familiarity with the table headings and notes can prevent errors caused by using the wrong pressure, length, or system condition.

Plan-reading and isometric questions may involve piping layouts, symbols, fittings, branches, offsets, elevations, and changes in direction. Trade-math questions may require work with fractions, decimals, measurements, conversions, geometry, angles, pipe lengths, and practical pipefitting calculations.

Safety preparation should include hazard recognition, personal protective equipment, hand and power tools, material handling, ladder safety, fire prevention, excavation awareness, housekeeping, and safe jobsite behavior. Gas piping professionals must understand how safe practices reduce the risk of injury, property damage, fire, and exposure to hazardous conditions.

Open Book Test

The Delaware Master HVACR Restricted – Gas Piping examination is an open-book test. Candidates may use approved references during the examination, subject to the testing provider’s current rules regarding books, highlighting, permanent tabs, handwritten material, inserts, and loose papers.

An open-book examination still requires focused preparation. There may not be enough time to search every book from beginning to end for every question. Candidates must know which reference is most likely to contain the answer and how the information is organized within that reference.

All rental books included in this package are highlighted and tabbed. The tabs help identify useful sections and subject areas, while the highlighting draws attention to important definitions, tables, code requirements, formulas, and safety concepts. These features are most effective when combined with regular practice using the books.

When studying a course lesson, locate the related information in the appropriate reference. For example, a lesson involving piping materials or system sizing should be reinforced by finding the applicable chapters and tables in the International Fuel Gas Code. A calculation lesson should be paired with practice in Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters.

Develop a consistent reference-search method. Begin by identifying the subject of the question, choose the most relevant book, use the index or table of contents, and then read the entire applicable section. Avoid selecting an answer based only on a highlighted phrase when the surrounding rule, exception, note, or table limitation may change the result.

Timed practice is also important. Track how long it takes to find information and record the topics that slow you down. Repeated navigation can help you recognize where commonly tested material is located and improve your ability to move between references efficiently.

Licensing Steps

The Delaware Board of Plumbing, Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Examiners regulates Master HVACR and Master HVACR Restricted licenses. Applicants pursuing the Gas Piping restricted specialty must complete the state’s licensing process and receive approval before sitting for the master examination.

  1. Confirm the appropriate classification. Select the Master HVACR Restricted license and identify Gas Piping as the specialty you intend to pursue.
  2. Create or access a DELPROS account. Delaware professional license applications are submitted through the Delaware Professional Regulation Online Services system.
  3. Complete the online application. Provide the requested personal, apprenticeship, employment, experience, examination, and licensing information.
  4. Gather supporting documents. Required materials may include apprenticeship records, journeyman credentials, employment verification, experience documentation, and license verifications from other jurisdictions.
  5. Submit the application and attachments. Follow the DELPROS instructions for documents that must be uploaded or provided by an employer, supervisor, school, licensing agency, or other third party.
  6. Complete any required preliminary examination. Applicants who did not complete the qualifying apprenticeship pathway may be required to satisfy an apprenticeship equivalency or bypass examination requirement.
  7. Receive Board approval to test. The Board must review and approve the applicant before the master examination can be scheduled.
  8. Register with the testing provider. Follow the scheduling instructions supplied after approval.
  9. Take the required examination. Bring only permitted materials and follow all current identification, reference-book, calculator, and testing-center rules.
  10. Complete outstanding licensing requirements. The Board must receive the required examination result and supporting documentation before issuing a license.

State Requirements

Delaware offers both an unrestricted Master HVACR license and several Master HVACR Restricted license specialties. The Gas Piping classification limits the license holder to the services authorized within that specialty. Contractors who plan to provide services across multiple HVACR specialties may need to pursue the unrestricted Master HVACR classification.

Applicants must satisfy the experience and training pathway applicable to their circumstances. A candidate who completed a qualifying apprenticeship program and received the appropriate journeyman credential may be required to document additional experience obtained under qualified supervision after completing that pathway.

Candidates without the qualifying apprenticeship credential may need to document a longer period of supervised HVACR or restricted HVACR experience and complete an apprenticeship equivalency or bypass examination when required. Employment and experience records must demonstrate that the applicant performed work relevant to the license classification being requested.

Official documents may need to be submitted by employers, supervisors, apprenticeship programs, schools, or licensing agencies. Applicants should begin gathering these records early so that missing documentation does not delay Board review.

The Delaware Board reviews completed applications and determines whether each applicant satisfies the state’s education, apprenticeship, employment, experience, and examination requirements. Purchasing this Ultimate Exam Prep Rental Package does not replace any state requirement and does not guarantee approval to test or issuance of a professional license.

A Delaware professional license is separate from a Delaware business license. Individuals who plan to operate a contracting business may also need business registration, tax accounts, insurance, local permits, and other credentials based on the services offered and the structure of the business.

Test Information and Study Materials

Create a study schedule that divides the examination material into manageable categories. Separate sessions can be devoted to fuel gas code navigation, piping installation, sizing, venting, LP gas, safety, mathematics, and plan reading. Give additional review time to subjects that require longer reference searches or repeated calculations.

Use the online course to establish a structured progression through the material. After completing a lesson, reinforce it by locating the related sections in the included books. This combination of instruction and active reference work can improve both technical understanding and exam-day navigation.

Practice sizing problems using the code tables. Confirm that you understand the table conditions, measurement method, developed length, demand, and applicable pressure before selecting an answer. Record mistakes and identify whether they were caused by the wrong table, incorrect arithmetic, or misreading the question.

Trade mathematics should be reviewed consistently. Work through calculations step by step, label units, and avoid skipping conversions. Frequent practice helps make mathematical procedures more familiar and reduces the likelihood of avoidable errors.

Complete timed practice sessions as the exam approaches. Answer familiar questions first, mark questions requiring longer calculations or searches, and return to them afterward. Read for terms such as minimum, maximum, required, permitted, prohibited, most appropriate, and least appropriate.

Before testing, review the current candidate instructions provided with your authorization and appointment. Confirm the testing location or delivery method, arrival requirements, identification rules, approved references, calculator policy, and any restrictions that apply to book preparation.
