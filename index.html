What do people ask before buying?

Which HVAC license do I need?

The right license depends on your state and the work you do. Each state page lists the HVAC, refrigeration and heating licenses we have prep for, who issues them and what each exam covers. Start with your state on the map.

Do I need EPA 608 certification for a state HVAC license?

They are separate. EPA Section 608 is a federal certification for anyone who handles refrigerant. Your state or city license has its own trade exam and often a business and law exam. Most working HVAC contractors hold both.

Is the HVAC contractor exam open book?

Many are. The Arizona C-39 and Oklahoma HVACR exams are open book, for example, and several Nevada, Connecticut and Idaho exams allow approved books. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed.

What is the difference between the book package and highlighted and tabbed books?

The book package is the full set of references for your exam. The highlighted and tabbed version is the same set, already marked up by instructors so you can find answers fast during an open-book exam.

What do the Ultimate Package and The 1 Package include?

The Ultimate Package bundles highlighted and tabbed rental books, a year of online course access and help with your license application. The 1 Package adds LLC or corporation setup, EIN filing and compliance guidance.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Licensing exam questions are confidential. The courses and practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

Does buying a package register me for the exam?

No. Your state board or city approves your application first, then the testing provider schedules the exam. A package gets you ready for the test itself.
